# HISTORY IDEAS — what real harems and palace dramas had that Cung Tường Mộng lacks
Owner 15/11 20:05Z #15 (history-expert worker). Branch `review/idea-history`, based on origin/main (fetched 15/11). No code, ideas only.

**Method.** Every idea names a source (dynastic history, regulation compilation, memoir, or a named drama). Where a fact is anecdote or disputed, it says so. "In game?" comes from `git grep` on `dev/build/j*.js` (origin/main) plus the design files; "missing" means no hit for the obvious Vietnamese terms (cited), so a reviewer should spot-check before building.
**Balance rule (owner 20:30Z)** is applied per idea: every bạc road has a second road of equal pain (Sức, Đức, ân tình debt, risk, time, favour owed), valued at her rank.
**Size:** S = one event chain or rule tweak; M = new small system or 5+ events; L = touches several systems (kids, map, calendar, triều đình).
**Ranking:** fun × fit (fit = matches existing systems and open owner asks: 20:05Z #1 Thái hậu inflation, #2 personality, #4 skimming, #5 poisoning the Emperor, 19:15Z Thái y question).

## Ranked ideas

### 1. Thái hậu archetypes drive thỉnh an (S–M) — fixes owner #1
- **History:** Empresses Dowager differed sharply: Hiếu Trang (Kangxi's grandmother, a steadying adviser; Thanh sử cảo, Hậu phi truyện), Hiếu Thánh Hiến (Qianlong's mother, pious, loved travel, went on his southern tours; Thanh sử cảo), Lữ thái hậu (cruel to rivals; Sử ký, Lữ thái hậu bản kỷ), Từ Dũ (Nguyễn, strict about virtue and frugality; Đại Nam liệt truyện), Từ Hi (controlling). Drama: Chân Hoàn truyện's Thái hậu is cold and political.
- **In game:** thỉnh an gives a flat high gain (owner 20:05Z #1); Thái hậu has no archetype.
- **Play:** each life rolls one Thái hậu archetype tied to her "trọng" virtue (owner #2: Hiếu/Lễ/Tiết/Kiệm…). Daily thỉnh an becomes a small test of that virtue: the frugal one frowns at new jewellery; the pious one rewards chép kinh and fasting days; the harsh one punishes lateness and finds fault on bad-mood days. Gains vary 0 to +8 and can go negative; ignoring her trọng costs.
- **Balance:** a gift that matches her trọng (bạc) vs. a costly act of that virtue (fasting = Sức, copying sutras = time slots). Equal value by rank.

### 2. "Thỉnh bình an mạch" — routine pulse check (S) — answers owner 19:15Z
- **History:** Qing Thái y viện doctors took routine "peace pulses" (請平安脈) of the Emperor, Thái hậu and consorts and wrote them in pulse records (mạch án); these Qing palace medical archives survive and are published (e.g. Chen Keji ed., *Thanh cung y án nghiên cứu* 清宮醫案研究).
- **In game:** Thái y exists in ~50 files, pregnancy is announced by events; no player act to request a pulse check (0 hits for "bình an mạch/thỉnh mạch").
- **Play:** a room act "Mời Thái y thỉnh mạch": reveals pregnancy early (before rivals know), health/hidden poison, fertility hints. Risk: the doctor belongs to someone's faction (owner's personality/faction system) and leaks the news; the record (mạch án) can later be forged against her. A free monthly routine check also comes automatically at Tần+ (rank privilege), but it is written in the official record, so everyone learns.
- **Balance:** private doctor = bạc; free official check = public news (rivals act sooner), which is the equal cost.

### 3. The trap dress — sumptuary colours as a weapon (M)
- **History:** Qing dress code by rank (*Hoàng triều lễ khí đồ thức* 皇朝禮器圖式, 1759): minh hoàng for Hoàng thái hậu/Hoàng hậu/Hoàng quý phi, kim hoàng for Quý phi and Phi, hương sắc for Tần. Drama: in Chân Hoàn truyện the Hoàng hậu has Chân Hoàn led into wearing the late Thuần Nguyên hoàng hậu's dress at a palace banquet, which ruins her.
- **In game:** over-rank attire is detected ("vượt phẩm", j92); no one can USE it against her.
- **Play:** a rival or a "friendly" maid sends a beautiful cloth/dress before a feast. Choices: wear it (big Sắc/Sủng if it is innocent; disaster if it is over-rank or a dead consort's), have it checked (Y/Tâm cơ check or ask the Nội vụ phủ), return it politely (offends the giver), or turn the trap back on the sender. She can also set this trap on NPCs.
- **Balance:** checking via a bribed Nội vụ phủ clerk (bạc) vs. checking via her own Tâm cơ check + one action slot + risk of a wrong verdict.

### 4. Ban Tiệp dư declines the Emperor's palanquin (S)
- **History:** Hán thư, Ngoại thích truyện: Emperor Cheng invited Ban Tiệp dư to ride in his palanquin; she refused, saying old paintings show sage rulers with great ministers beside them, only the last kings of the Three Dynasties with favourites. Thái hậu Vương praised her.
- **In game:** missing (0 hits "cùng kiệu/chung kiệu").
- **Play:** at an outing the Emperor offers his palanquin. Accept: Sủng up, jealousy, Thái hậu/officials frown (Danh down if Thái hậu archetype values Lễ). Refuse with the Ban Tiệp dư line (Thi/Tài check to phrase it well): Đức + Thái hậu favour; the Emperor's reaction depends on his personality (a romantic Emperor is hurt).
- **Balance:** no bạc; the two roads trade Sủng vs. Đức/Thái hậu at equal weight.

### 5. Tùy giá — who goes with the Emperor (summer palace, autumn hunt, southern tour) (L)
- **History:** Qing emperors spent summers at the Tị thử sơn trang (Chengde) and Viên Minh Viên, held the autumn hunt at Mộc Lan (木蘭秋獮), and Kangxi and Qianlong each made six southern tours; selected consorts went along. On the 1765 southern tour Qianlong's Empress Ô Lạt Na Lạp cut her hair after a quarrel and fell (Thanh sử cảo, Hậu phi truyện). Dramas: Như Ý truyện (southern tour), Diên Hi công lược (hunt).
- **In game:** princes' autumn hunt only (j53 `san`); "hành cung" 1 hit; no consort travel selection.
- **Play:** a few times a year the travel list is drawn up. Getting on it: Sủng, Danh, gifts to the Kính sự phòng head, or the Thái hậu's word. On the trip there are new places, fewer rivals and closer time with the Emperor. Staying behind: she can run things at home (Đức, schemes while the strong ones are away). Rare event: the hair-cutting quarrel as a dramatic exit.
- **Balance:** bribe the list-keeper (bạc) vs. ask Thái hậu/Hoàng hậu (owed favour, repaid later in a hard event).

### 6. Low-rank mothers may not raise their own child (L)
- **History:** In the Qing palace a child born to a low-rank consort was often raised by a higher one. The Yongzheng Emperor was raised by Consort Đồng (Tong Jiashi, later Hiếu Ý Nhân hoàng hậu), not his birth mother Ô Nhã thị (Thanh sử cảo). Drama: in Chân Hoàn truyện princess Ôn Nghi goes to Đoan phi after Tào quý nhân dies.
- **In game:** one event where Thái hậu asks someone to adopt a motherless princess; no rank rule.
- **Play:** if she gives birth below Tần (PAL_HEAD, j1), a chủ vị takes the child. She can pick among patrons (alliance and a hostage at once), petition to keep the child (Đức + Thái hậu test), or win a promotion first. At high rank she can take in a low consort's child herself: an heir, but an enemy for life. The child's loyalty is split (kids system j53).
- **Balance:** a gift to the chosen patron (bạc) vs. a long-term debt (that patron can call it in once).

### 7. The hidden prince (L)
- **History:** Minh sử, Hậu phi truyện: under the Chenghua Emperor, with Vạn Quý phi feared for ending pregnancies, Lady Kỷ's son (the future Hongzhi Emperor) was hidden for about six years with the help of eunuch Trương Mẫn and the deposed Empress Ngô, then revealed to the Emperor; his mother died soon after.
- **In game:** schemes against pregnancies exist (musk, catswap); no road for hiding a child.
- **Play:** when a strong rival is known to harm pregnancies, she can hide the pregnancy and child: needs a loyal eunuch, a quiet place (lãnh cung ally), and monthly secrecy checks. Revealing at the right moment (Emperor longing for an heir) gives a huge reward; discovery means a deadly scandal.
- **Balance:** paying for silence each month (bạc) vs. loyalty (servant loyalty spent, risk each tuần).

### 8. Seasonal ice and charcoal by rank (S–M) — extends stipend skimming (owner #4)
- **History:** Qing per-rank palace allowances (cung phần) are listed in *Quốc triều cung sử* 國朝宮史 (Qianlong era), including charcoal grades; the Ming eunuch office Tích tân ty supplied smokeless "hồng la than" (Lưu Nhược Ngu, *Chước trung chí* 酌中志). Summer ice came from the palace ice cellars (still standing in the Forbidden City). Drama: withheld charcoal for out-of-favour consorts is a common palace-drama beat.
- **In game:** stipend text has "than" (j20 `BLG`), one "than sưởi" event (j14), heat/ice weather text (j68f); no grade, no summer ice.
- **Play:** winter: good charcoal (no smoke) or black smoky charcoal (Sức down, cough, the child falls ill). Summer: an ice share, more at high rank. Skimmers take it first. Choices: endure, bribe, ask a patron to share hers (owed favour), burn her own fine incense/charcoal from the chest (items).
- **Balance:** bribe the office (bạc) vs. a patron's share (ân tình debt) vs. endure (Sức and health risk).

### 9. Hiệp lý lục cung as a real job (M–L)
- **History:** after Empress Phú Sát died (1748) Qianlong gave Ô Lạt Na Lạp the "nhiếp lục cung sự" power (Thanh sử cảo). Drama: Hoa phi holds 協理六宮 in Chân Hoàn truyện.
- **In game:** only a rare-ending reward (j97 "Hiệp lý lục cung").
- **Play:** at Quý phi+, when the Hoàng hậu is ill/absent, she may be granted co-management: a monthly docket of 2–3 cases (stipend lists, a theft, a quarrel between consorts, a maid's punishment). She can be fair (Đức, Danh), favour allies, or skim others' stipends (mirror of owner's skimming feature). Mistakes are reported to the Thái hậu.
- **Balance:** the docket can be settled by paying compensation (bạc) or by a judgement that angers one side (relation cost).

### 10. Poisoning the Emperor must beat real locks (M–L) — owner #5
- **History:** Lễ ký, Khúc lễ hạ: "when the ruler is ill and drinks medicine, the minister tastes it first". Tang *Đường lục điển* (Thượng dược cục) records the tasting chain for imperial medicine. Precedent of failure: Nhâm Dần cung biến (1542), palace women led by Dương Kim Anh tried to strangle the Jiajing Emperor; all, including Ninh tần Vương thị and the (probably innocent) Đoan phi Tào thị, were executed (Minh sử).
- **In game:** jpoison.js exists; the Emperor and Thái hậu are off-limits (owner wants this opened).
- **Play:** three locks: taster, two-pot rule (one portion tasted), and the record of who touched the bowl. Each lock is opened by bribery, by an ally (the Vương gia line), or by time (a slow poison over months, mimicking illness). Failure: the Nhâm Dần fate for her and everyone she used.
- **Balance:** each lock has a bạc road and a people/time road of equal risk.

### 11. Court painter's portrait (M)
- **History:** *Tây Kinh tạp ký* (anecdote, not in Hán thư): Han Yuandi chose women by portraits; the others bribed the painter Mao Diên Thọ; Vương Chiêu Quân refused, was painted plain and sent to the Xiongnu. Real later case: Giuseppe Castiglione (Lang Thế Ninh) painted Qianlong, the Empress and eleven consorts in the scroll *Tâm tả trị bình* (Cleveland Museum of Art).
- **In game:** "chân dung" only as an art product; no portrait event.
- **Play:** the painter comes to paint all consorts. Bribe him for a flattering portrait; sit through long sessions charming him with talk (Tài check, Sức); or refuse. A plain portrait lowers Sủng now but makes her a hòa thân candidate (link to nc / rare endings); a fine one hangs in the album and gives Danh for years.
- **Balance:** bribe (bạc) vs. long sittings (Sức + 2 slots) + a check that can fail.

### 12. Phùng Chiêu nghi faces the bear (S–M)
- **History:** Hán thư, Ngoại thích truyện: at a beast show a bear broke loose toward Han Yuandi; Phùng Chiêu nghi stepped in front of it while Phó Chiêu nghi fled. Phó later took revenge with a witchcraft charge and Phùng died.
- **In game:** missing (0 hits "gấu/mãnh thú").
- **Play:** a sudden event at a hunt or beast show with a short timer: step in front (Sức cost, injury chance, big Sủng/Danh), shield a child, push a rival forward, or flee. A rival who fled hates her and plans the witchcraft frame later (links existing j42 plant scheme).
- **Balance:** no bạc; Sức and risk vs. safety.

### 13. Birth rites: tắm ba ngày, đầy tháng, thôi nôi (M)
- **History:** the first-birthday object grab is described in Nhan Chi Thôi's *Nhan thị gia huấn* (6th c.); Hồng Lâu Mộng ch. 2 has Bảo Ngọc grabbing rouge; Vietnamese thôi nôi uses the same tray. The third-day bath (洗三) was a Qing palace and Beijing custom.
- **In game:** missing (0 hits "thôi nôi/đầy tháng").
- **Play:** three short ceremonies with guests and gifts (who comes = a map of her friends). Thôi nôi: a tray of brush, book, sword, seal, abacus, rouge; the grab nudges the child's aptitude and the Emperor's hopes. She can place the item near the child (Tâm cơ; if seen, Đức down and gossip).
- **Balance:** a lavish banquet (bạc) vs. a modest one that earns Đức from a frugal Thái hậu (archetype link).

### 14. Choosing the wet nurse (M)
- **History:** Ming: Tianqi's nurse Khách thị, titled Phụng Thánh phu nhân, ruled the palace with eunuch Ngụy Trung Hiền (Minh sử). Qing: Kangxi's nurse Tôn thị (mother of Tào Dần) kept favour for life.
- **In game:** nhũ mẫu only as an observer of the child's aptitude (j53 `nzNurse`).
- **Play:** the Nội vụ phủ offers 3 nurses, each with a hidden patron. The chosen one shapes the child and can become a spy or her strongest ally; if her son becomes Emperor, the nurse becomes a power she must handle.
- **Balance:** vetting via family contacts (bạc) vs. her own Tâm cơ watch over months.

### 15. Mourning period beyond clothes (M)
- **History:** Han Văn đế's will shortened mourning by "a day for a month" (Hán thư, Văn đế kỷ); later courts banned music, feasts and bright colours during quốc tang.
- **In game:** only attire (j92 `tangPunish`).
- **Play:** after a Thái hậu/Emperor death: no lật thẻ, festivals cancelled on the calendar, no music. A pregnancy conceived in mourning is a scandal (link to the Kính sự phòng book). Grief acts (keeping vigil, copying sutras) earn Đức; sneaking a meeting earns Sủng and huge risk.
- **Balance:** paying for memorial rites (bạc) vs. keeping vigil (Sức + slots).

### 16. Fake pregnancy, false diagnosis (S–M)
- **History/drama:** Chân Hoàn truyện: Thẩm Mi Trang is made to believe she is pregnant by a bribed doctor (Lưu Bạn) and then accused of faking.
- **In game:** "giả thai" 0 hits; doctors can catch faked illness (j13 "ngươi giả bệnh").
- **Play:** a rival fakes a pregnancy to win favour, or a bribed doctor tells HER she is pregnant. Uncover: a second doctor, her own Y art, or watching the rival's kitchen. Being fooled ends in a public "deception" charge.
- **Balance:** a second doctor (bạc) vs. her own Y check + time.

### 17. Giám quốc while the Emperor is at war (M)
- **History:** Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư: in 1069, while Lý Thánh Tông campaigned against Champa, Nguyên phi Ỷ Lan ran the state so well the people praised her, and he returned resolved to win.
- **In game:** only "buông rèm" as an ending (j3).
- **Play:** a rare season-long event at high Sủng/Danh: she handles memorials from the court (j55 triều đình clans). Good judgement: huge Danh, a title. Overreach: "hậu cung can chính" charge (existing can chính rules).
- **Balance:** smoothing ministers with gifts (bạc) vs. favours promised to a clan (j55 power shift).

### 18. Lập hậu — the ruyi and the pouches (M)
- **History:** late-Qing anecdote (widely retold, details uncertain): at Guangxu's 1888 selection the ruyi went to the Empress (Long Dụ) and embroidered pouches to the consorts (Cẩn, Trân), with the Thái hậu steering the choice.
- **In game:** "kim sách kim ấn" only in the ending text (j3); no contest when the Empress seat empties.
- **Play:** when the Hoàng hậu dies or is deposed, a court-wide contest: the Emperor's favour, the Thái hậu's pick, and clan votes (j55). Getting the pouch instead of the ruyi still gives a Quý phi rank.
- **Balance:** clan lobbying (bạc) vs. Thái hậu favour spent.

### 19. Her own phong hiệu (S)
- **History:** Qing consorts at Tần and above got a title character (e.g. Lệnh phi 令妃, Thuần phi 純妃), recorded in Thanh sử cảo; the character was meaningful. Drama: Chân Hoàn's titles.
- **In game:** phong hiệu exists only for children (j67) and one rare reward (j97).
- **Play:** at Tần the Emperor picks one of 3 characters from her top virtue/art; it shows in her rank line and gives a small permanent bonus. Rivals can spread a bad homophone reading.
- **Balance:** none needed (no bạc).

### 20. Naming taboo and the literary trap (S)
- **History:** Nguyễn court: the word "hoa" was read "huê" to avoid the name of Thiệu Trị's mother Hồ Thị Hoa. Qing văn tự ngục: poems punished for a wrong character (Qianlong era, e.g. the Hồ Trung Tảo case, 1755).
- **In game:** missing (0 hits "kiêng húy").
- **Play:** a poem or embroidered text for a contest can contain a taboo word; a rival reports it (Đức down, cấm túc). Her check: Tài/Thi, or a literate maid proofreads. She can plant one in a rival's entry.
- **Balance:** a paid scribe (bạc) vs. a maid's time/loyalty + check.

### 21. Trường Môn phú — buying a poem from the cold palace (S)
- **History:** the preface of *Trường Môn phú* (in *Văn tuyển*) says Empress Trần A Kiều paid Tư Mã Tương Như 100 cân vàng for it; many scholars think the preface is later, and she was never restored.
- **In game:** Trường Môn viện is the lãnh cung name (j27); no "plea poem" road.
- **Play:** from lãnh cung or disfavour: commission a famous poem, write her own, or have a maid sing it where the Emperor walks. Odds are honest ("Năm ăn năm thua") because history says it failed.
- **Balance:** commission (bạc via family) vs. her own poem (Tài check + Sức + many slots).

### 22. Thai giáo — prenatal teaching (S–M)
- **History:** Lưu Hướng, *Liệt nữ truyện*: Thái Nhậm, mother of King Văn, saw no evil sights and heard no lewd sounds while pregnant.
- **In game:** missing.
- **Play:** weekly pregnancy choices (classics, music, calm) slightly raise the child's starting aptitude; scheming while pregnant lowers its Đức seed. A virtue-loving Thái hậu notices.
- **Balance:** a tutor/musician (bạc) vs. her own time slots.

### 23. Emperor sends dishes (S)
- **History:** Qing imperial kitchen meal records (膳底檔, First Historical Archives) list dishes the Emperor sent from his table to named consorts.
- **In game:** one line about tribute lychees sent to her (j68g, jstip "vải thiều"); no system.
- **Play:** at dinner the Emperor sends a dish; who gets which dish, and first, is a public sign read by everyone. She can share it with a rival to calm her (Đức), or eat it alone (Sủng signal, jealousy).
- **Balance:** none (no bạc).

### 24. Succession and the army (M)
- **History:** Đặng Thị Huệ (Tuyên phi) got lord Trịnh Sâm to pass over his elder son for her son Trịnh Cán; after Sâm died in 1782 the Tam phủ soldiers (kiêu binh) overthrew Cán (Hoàng Lê nhất thống chí; Khâm định Việt sử thông giám cương mục).
- **In game:** succession outcomes (j3) and the secret edict (j97); no army reaction.
- **Play:** if her son is a younger son raised over an elder, the court/army factions (j55, dtq clan) may revolt when the Emperor dies. She must win generals, the elder son's mother, or ministers' "lập trưởng" argument.
- **Balance:** rewards to soldiers (bạc) vs. a marriage alliance (her daughter / a clan favour).

### 25. Asking to withdraw her tablet (S)
- **History:** eunuch memoirs on the Kính sự phòng describe consorts' tablets being withdrawn while ill or unwell.
- **In game:** Lật thẻ and a tablet-keeper eunuch exist; she cannot withdraw on purpose.
- **Play:** "Cáo bệnh rút thẻ" for a few days to rest Sức, dodge a trap night or a rival's plan. Cost: Sủng decay and "thất sủng" gossip. Enemies can bribe the keeper to "lose" her tablet.
- **Balance:** paying the keeper to put it back early (bạc) vs. a public reason (illness) that Thái y must confirm.

### 26. Ban sóc — receiving next year's calendar (S) — links the month calendar
- **History:** the Nguyễn court held lễ Ban sóc at Ngọ Môn on the 1st of the 12th month, issuing the Hiệp Kỷ calendar (Khâm định Đại Nam hội điển sự lệ).
- **In game:** month calendar planned (kitMonthCal, jmcal); no year-end issue rite.
- **Play:** each year-end the court issues the new calendar; next year's festivals unlock on her wall calendar; a Khâm thiên giám note hints at a good/bad month.
- **Balance:** none.

### 27. Banquet seating puzzle (S–M)
- **History:** Qing New Year's Eve family banquets at Càn Thanh cung seated consorts strictly by rank (documented in Qing court records and Palace Museum publications).
- **In game:** exam has protocol questions (jexam); quarrels over seats near the Thái hậu (j71 "tranh nhau chỗ ngồi").
- **Play:** when she co-manages (idea 9) or hosts a party, she arranges the table; wrong order offends; a clever seat can humiliate a rival on purpose. Also a protocol exam minigame.
- **Balance:** a fixed-fee master of rites (bạc) vs. her own Lễ check.

### 28. Nữ quan bureaus for the maid road (M)
- **History:** Tang *Đường lục điển*: six bureaus of women officials (Thượng cung, Thượng nghi, Thượng phục, Thượng thực, Thượng tẩm, Thượng công).
- **In game:** the servant ladder ends at "Nữ quan" with no specialty.
- **Play:** the cung nữ road picks a bureau at Nữ quan: Thượng thực (poison tasting power), Thượng phục (wardrobe, can sabotage dresses), Thượng nghi (rites, exams). Each gives unique acts.
- **Balance:** exam fees (bạc) vs. longer service time.

### 29. Đế hệ thi naming (S)
- **History:** Minh Mạng's *Đế hệ thi* gave generation characters (Miên, Hường, Ưng, Bửu, Vĩnh…); Qing used generation characters too (Dận, Hoằng, Vĩnh, Miên).
- **In game:** no naming of her child (only Ngọc điệp in a j97 ending).
