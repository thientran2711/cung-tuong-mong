# ROBOT PLAYER REVIEW — standing order for EVERY robot life (owner 25/11 ~05:50Z, OD row verbatim)
Applies to every robot runner (solo + co-op, every account), together with: real taps (OD ~05:10Z), life until death/real ending, no cap (~05:15Z), no voluntary exit unless it is the only option (~05:20Z/~05:40Z).
The runner session (the AI driving the robot) plays as a REAL PLAYER and GAME REVIEWER, in its persona (bot profile → player type: random=casual newcomer, skilled=core gamer, greedy=min-maxer, schemer=drama/intrigue lover, cautious=careful planner, lover=romance player, speed=impatient mobile player, scholar=lore/story reader, rebel=rule-breaker, lazy=low-effort player, cnv_mat/thd_tho=as named).

## A. DURING PLAY — moment log (dev/handover/robot/review/<run>-<life>-moments.md)
- Look at the real screen (Fold 755x1000 shot) at least every 20 buổi AND at every new screen/frame type, every mini-game, every big event (rank change, pregnancy, death of a person, scheme result, ending).
- Write a moment line whenever you feel: CONFUSED (don't know what to do / what a button does), BORED (same card/event again, nothing happens), FRUSTRATED (dead/slow button, unfair loss, too many taps), DELIGHTED (good scene, satisfying win), ODD (story/logic does not make sense, wrong name/person, wording not cổ trang, numbers that disagree). Format: game date · screen · feeling · what happened · shot path.

## B. END-OF-LIFE REVIEW (dev/handover/robot/review/<run>-<life>-review.md) — one per life
1. Summary: persona, origin/road, years, max rank, ending, real minutes.
2. Score 1–10 + 2–4 sentences each, with concrete examples (date/screen):
   a. First 15 minutes (do I understand the goal, the screens, what to do next?)
   b. Gameplay loop & pacing (fun each buổi? too slow/fast? repetition?)
   c. Buttons & UI (every dead/wrong/unclear/too-small button; taps to do a thing; frames that cannot close)
   d. Story & drama (does the plot hold, do events follow from my choices, any nonsense?)
   e. Characters & interactions (do people feel alive, react to what I did, remember, act on their own; two-way law)
   f. Choices & fairness (equal-value choices? any option clearly best or cost-only? harder as I climb?)
   g. Rewards, feedback & emotion (do wins/losses feel earned and shown? sounds/animations)
   h. Mini-games (fun, fair, right difficulty for my rank, only when I do it myself)
   i. Visuals & sound (art, figures, makeup, music stopping, odd sounds)
   j. Co-op (co-op runs only: sync, waiting, feeling of playing together)
3. TOP 3 things I loved · TOP 3 things I hated · Would I play another life? why.
4. BUGS found (each: repro, shot, save) — these go to the bug fast lane at once.
5. Suggestions (as a player). NOTE feature freeze (law 18): suggestions are recorded, MAIN files new-feature ones in dev/PARKING_LOT.md; bugs / broken UX are fixed now.

## C. DIGEST (per line, every ~10 lives) — reports/robot-review-digest-<line>.md
Merge reviews: average score per area, recurring themes ranked by how many lives hit them (with example quotes), bug list, suggestion list. MAIN merges the line digests into dev/handover/reports/ROBOT_PLAYER_REVIEW.md and tells sếp the top findings in Vietnamese.
Player-visible quotes stay in Vietnamese; report text English.
