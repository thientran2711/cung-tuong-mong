# MAIN ROOT-72 → Foreman C: «hành động» (act) vs «biểu cảm» (feeling) seals — answer to the owner's question (20/11)
Source of truth: dev/build/jfeel.js header §5 + BAL.feel.act (owner OD 17/11 ~20:45Z: 17 acts, ONE per feeling, Bình thản none) and jemoact EA_NACT (role reactions).
## (1) Difference in the game
- FEELING seal (biểu cảm) = the person's MOOD shown on the face, any time the feeling is live (light/middle/heavy level). Face only.
- ACT seal (HĐ·x, face key "act-<feeling>") = shown ONLY while the person is DOING the act her heavy feeling pushed her into (it lasts its khắc: 1 khắc, 2 khắc or the rest of the buổi; BAL.feel.act.kh). Priority: active ACT > strongest FEELING. So the act picture must show the DEED (pose + hands + prop + where she is), not a mood face — a viewer must be able to tell "she is doing X" without the caption. If an act picture only shows a face, it duplicates the feeling and is wrong.
