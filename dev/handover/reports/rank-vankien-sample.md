- Vân kiên 云肩: flat cloud-shaped shoulder collar (Yuan→Qing, women's ceremonial dress). Size stays near shoulder width; rank shows in embroidery, not bulk.
- Grows with rank: embroidery density, gold-thread share, pearl rows and tassel fringe, phoenix motif count, headdress (hairpin → phoenix hairpin → full phoenix crown with pearl strands).

- Palace dramas (Zhen Huan-style, Ruyi's Royal Love, Story of Yanxi) show rank mainly via headdress, embroidery colour/gold, phoenix reserved for empress; costume bulk is not the signal. (From general knowledge, no web check.)
