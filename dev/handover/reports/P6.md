## Stylist verdict per group (did toning down keep the pairs beautiful?)
- **Dịu dàng** (light + soft): yes. Mains drop from C*45 to 37 and read like silk in soft daylight. Pink + cream / mist still works.
  The danger was Hạ: soft peach on cream reads washed out and beige-on-beige. Hạ now gets a clear, cool light phụ
  (xanh sương, bạc hà, xanh trời dịu, tím hoa cà dịu) or lemon + mist. I removed every deep colour that P5's gate had let in (c02 Hạ tím than).
- **Đoan trang** (c06 formula): this group looks most like the owner's model. Soft mid mains C*38 + ngà / trắng ngà / hồng phấn.
  Changed phụ: cream / ivory / peach cream under soft orange or lemon mains (beige on orange looks MUDDY at soft chroma) became
  hồng phấn, xanh sương, bạc hà, or a soft colour phụ in Hạ (ngọc dịu, xanh trời dịu).
- **Sắc sảo** (deep + light contrast): yes, as long as the phụ is very light. Deep crimson, plum, sapphire and wine at C*40 with trắng ngà /
  xanh sương / hồng phấn (ΔL ≥ .34 enforced) stay sharp without shouting. In Hạ the main cannot be deep, so the
  DEPTH MOVES TO THE PHỤ: cam san hô / chanh + lam ngọc trầm / tím than / xanh ngọc sẫm ("~đậm: phụ"). That gives the sharp
  contrast back. Stylist favourite of the set.
- **Kiêu kỳ** (deep jewel + light gold/ivory): good in Xuân/Thu/Đông (đỏ rượu thẫm, tím mận, xanh lá thẫm, tím than + ngà / vàng nhạt /
  kem). Hạ uses the same depth-in-the-phụ trick. Weak: vàng nhạt / ngà / kem chanh are still one ΔE cluster, so most Kiêu kỳ get
  vàng nhạt or ngà once a year and a cool mist the other seasons.
- **Lạnh lùng** (cool clear mid-light): yes. Softened ice, lilac and jade + mist phụ read clean, not grey (floor C* 24, no grey neutrals).
  This group had the most phụ changes (36) because cool mist phụ are few and A31 reshuffled them.
- **Quyến rũ** (rich warm): it lost the most chroma (63 to 42.5), so "rực" became "nồng nàn, đậm đà": đỏ thắm, đỏ rượu trầm, san hô +
  cam hồng / đào / hồng san hô. Still the warmest, richest group on the sheet. In Hạ it takes deep accents (xanh ngọc trầm, lam ngọc trầm).
