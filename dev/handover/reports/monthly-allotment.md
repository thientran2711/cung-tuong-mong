## 1. History first (law 8, OD:L1327)
Base research already exists and is reused, not redone: dev/handover/reports/monthly-ration-history.md (Foreman B expert, 17/11: 分例 structure, Qing figures, cosmetics, tribute, skimming, dramas). Below only what that report did not cover and this job needs: the lines beyond tea/cloth/cosmetics, and the SERVANTS' allotment. Confidence tags as there: [A] well attested · [B] commonly cited, numbers vary · [F] fiction · [?] needs checking.
- **Structure of a Qing 宮分 (cung phần) list** [A]: 《國朝宮史》 卷十七–十九 "宮分" lists, rank by rank, a yearly silver sum, bolts of named silks/satins, then DAILY food (pork, rice, flour, eggs, vegetables, tea leaves by named grade) and fuel/light: 紅羅炭 / 黑炭 charcoal (fine smokeless for high ranks, black coal for low) and **candles by grade: 白蠟 (white wax) for the top, 黃蠟 (yellow beeswax) for the middle, 羊油蠟 (mutton-tallow) for the low** [B on exact rank cut-offs]. So light is a real, rank-graded monthly line next to coal — the game had coal but no light.
- **Hair oil** [B]: 頭油 (đầu du) was a standing toilet line for palace women; the common kinds were 桂花油 (osmanthus oil, the better grade) and 刨花水 (water of soaked elm/wood shavings, the cheap everyday dressing that keeps hair glossy and flat), with jasmine/orchid scented oils for the top. 《宮女談往錄》 (late-Qing maid's memoir) describes maids combing with 刨花水 and buying better oil from their own pay. Hair oil matters for the elaborate court coiffure (兩把頭 / 大拉翅 in late Qing; any high, pinned style): no oil, no high style [B]. Game: an elaborate style (Kiểu cầu kỳ) needs hair oil.
- **Servants' allotment** [B]: palace maids (宮女) and eunuchs drew a small monthly silver (月銀) by grade plus rice, and **seasonal clothing** (四季衣裳: cloth/garments by season, coarse for the lowest), coal in winter in their quarters (coarse), and the common toilet goods (hair oil / powder of the cheapest kind) — better things came from their mistress (賞) or their own purse. A maid's allotment came THROUGH her mistress's palace: when the mistress fell out of favour, her people were squeezed too (the classic drama beat: the whole palace gets black coal) [A for the pattern in memoirs/dramas, B for detail]. Head maids (掌事姑姑) and female officials drew more and finer.
- **Law C (owner 08:25Z)**: as in the earlier report, rank buys a FINER grade, not more of it; the cost of the grade (price of buying extra) scales with rank. Same here: candle and hair-oil counts are flat, the grade rises.
- **Dramas** [F]: 《甄嬛傳》 — the disgraced palace gets black smoking coal; 《延禧攻略》 — maids share one bottle of hair oil, a gift of good oil wins a friend; 《如懿傳》 — an allowance cut reaches the maids first.
