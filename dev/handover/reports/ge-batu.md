## History expert note (law 8; Persian court / caravan life, short)
Setting = the Sasanian house in its last years (630s–670s): Đại Thực (the Arab caliphate) takes Tô Lận (蘇藺, Ctesiphon, Tùy thư) and the heartland; the royal line flees east; Peroz III reaches Chang'an and gets the Tang title Hữu vũ vệ tướng quân (used for Mật Hồ Đạt's thiện ending), his son Narsieh = Nê Niết Sư (泥涅師, Tang sources). The council of great houses (the seven/nine noble families) really chose and unseated kings; queens Bố Lan (Boran) and Azarmidokht truly reigned in 630–632 — so «Nữ vương Tô Lận» by «the old rule» is grounded. Court and faith: Vương trung vương (shahanshah), sacred fire kept in fire temples (Tang 祆祠 → Hỏa Tiên từ), oaths sworn before the fire (Mithra, keeper of oaths), the dead laid on a stone tower for the birds (dakhma), Nowruz at the spring equinox (Nô Nhụ, the song ending), Pahlavi script (Bát La Bà). Caravan life: Sogdian/Persian hồ thương on the Lương Châu – Ngọc Môn quan – Tây Thị road, silver drachms and camels as currency, winged emblems on seal rings, polo (chovgān = mã cầu) and royal hunts as the noble sport — so stable, polo and hunt endings (bd_san) and the refugee / grain market endings (bd_tuyet) sit in real practice. Words avoided: steppe, khả hãn, kim trướng, ovoo, wolf.
