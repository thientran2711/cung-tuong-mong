# Cung quy (palace etiquette) research — Qing harem rules vs. palace dramas

Research note for Cung Tường Mộng (rank-interaction rules). English work file; Vietnamese labels are
Sino-Vietnamese terms usable in player text (check against VANPHONG.md before use).

**Status legend (column "Kind")**
- **HIST** = supported by historical record or scholarship (archives, statutes, memoirs, academic work).
- **DRAMA** = drama convention (shared by 甄嬛传 / 延禧攻略 / 如懿传; fine for a game, not proven history).
- **MIX** = real institution, but drama exaggerates or formalises it.

**Honesty notes.** Web evidence on daily etiquette details (exact bows, speech forms, seat order) is thin:
most Chinese web pieces are pop-history. The strongest historical anchors are: the rank ladder and quotas,
the 主位 / side-hall residence system, demotion only by imperial decree, the 惇妃 1778 case, and the
mass-promotion pattern (Mao Liping & Shen Xin, 《壸政》). Episode numbers marked "(ep. ?)" were not verified
online and come from general knowledge of the plots; all others were confirmed in an episode guide.

Retinue by rank (Qianlong 14 rule): Thái hậu/Hoàng hậu 仪驾, Hoàng quý phi/Quý phi 仪仗, Phi/Tần 彩仗 (S1, search
summary of thenewslens 145584). Ming: Hoàng hậu, Hoàng quý phi, Quý phi, Phi, Tần plus many titled ladies (昭仪,
婕妤, 美人…); Ming had 8 Phi / 9 Tần, Qing cut to 4 / 6 (S1, ifeng 7skLTxMjRHg).
Promotion was historically **slow and collective** (普升 one step at accessions, empress investitures, imperial or
dowager birthdays); Qianlong said consorts "循序而进", not by favour. Single fast jumps (慈安, 慈禧 after bearing the
only son) were rare (S2). Dramas compress this into favour-driven jumps (DRAMA).

## Game-design takeaways (suggested, not owner-approved)
1. Rank gap decides **who may act on whom**: greet up, summon/visit/punish/reward down; equals only gift and bicker.
2. **主位** (Tần+) is a local boss over her side-hall residents; Empress (and a 协理 holder) is a global boss.
3. Every "upward" hostile act is **dĩ hạ phạm thượng** unless backed by favour; favour delays the penalty, it does not cancel it.
4. Promotion/demotion is an Emperor-only verb; others can only petition (đề nghị / tấu thỉnh).
5. Accusation upward needs evidence; failure triggers **vu cáo phản tọa**.
