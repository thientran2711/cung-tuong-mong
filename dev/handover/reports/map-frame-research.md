# map-frame-research — how others present a historical/wuxia/palace MAP screen (owner OD:L1327: expert looks at how others do it)
Context: rounds 1-3 of the Dư đồ frame were all rejected (ornate border, thin muted border, torn-leather sheet). Owner 16/11 20:05Z: "Dư đồ xấu quá ... cho chuyên gia đi coi anh em khác vẽ dư đồ như thế nào, game kiếm hiệp, cổ trang". Nothing copied: below only DESCRIBES approaches and links sources. No art of any game is reused.
Honest limit: my web tool returns TEXT, not screenshots, so I could check what articles/catalogues say, not look at each game's screen. Confidence tag per reference: [src] = stated in a linked source; [mine] = my own knowledge of how it looks, not verifiable here (the foreman/owner should eyeball screenshots before betting on those).

## References (what makes each look good)
1. **Total War: Three Kingdoms campaign map + UI** [src]. The studio says the look is "a very modern take ... inspired by a modern painter painting these historical landscapes, so it doesn't look like parchment"; the UI uses flowing/receding ink effects, "paper dipped in ink". Lesson: the whole screen is painted landscape, no parchment sheet, no frame; ink is the only edge treatment. Sources: [GamesBeat art interview](https://gamesbeat.com/how-creative-assembly-conceived-the-art-for-total-war-three-kingdoms/), [The Sixth Axis interview](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2018/08/16/interview-exploring-ancient-china-in-total-war-three-kingdoms/), [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/total-war-three-kingdoms/total-war-three-kingdoms-features).
2. **Japanese Rakuchu-rakugai-zu screens (Kyoto bird's-eye views)** [src]. Gold clouds "isolate buildings and districts ... in a golden net", Kyoto sites seen "through golden clouds", scalloped clouds partition scenes. Lesson: cloud/mist bands are the frame AND the divider between districts, and they hide where the picture stops. Very close to our subject (a bird's-eye of a palace compound). Sources: [Cornell](https://emuseum.cornell.edu/objects/26902/rakuchurakugai-in-and-around-kyoto), [Asian Art Museum guide](https://education.asianart.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2019/12/Japanese_folding_screens.pdf), [Cleveland Museum](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/683744).
3. **Qing imperial architectural drawings (Yangshi Lei archive), palace plans with yellow tags** [src]. Presentation drawings of Forbidden City buildings carry small paper slips: yellow slips name the building and give measures, red slips give advice. Lesson: hall names as small paper slips pasted on the drawing is an authentic palace-plan idiom (kit label parts could wear it). Sources: [National Palace Museum Taipei](https://theme.npm.edu.tw/exh112/QingArchitectural/en/page-2.html), [UNESCO](https://en.unesco.org/silkroad/silk-road-themes/documentary-heritage/qing-dynasty-yangshi-lei-archives), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yangshi_Lei_Archives).
4. **Chinese scroll mounting (handscroll / hanging scroll)** [src]. Silk-brocade border + a narrow gold strip + roller ends; three border formats. Lesson: this is exactly what our rounds 1-2 imitated (rectangular mount) and the owner rejected it, so "museum mount" is the wrong family for her. Source: [Orientations: mounting in the Qianlong court](https://www.orientations.com.hk/highlights/the-art-of-mounting-calligraphy-and-painting).
5. **Ghost of Tsushima / Yotei** [src, partial]. Minimal HUD, "no minimap", ink-like map reviewers compare to a colouring book; Sucker Punch worked with a sumi-e artist on the sequel. Lesson: a map that is simply the painting, with almost no chrome; guidance by world cues. Sources: [Tokyo Weekender](https://www.tokyoweekender.com/2020/07/what-sets-ghost-of-tsushima-apart-from-the-rest/), [CGMagazine](https://www.cgmagonline.com/interviews/tsushima-to-ghost-of-yotei/), [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/ghost-of-tsushima-design-analysis).
6. **Where Winds Meet (wuxia open world)** [src, partial]. Map starts mostly black with only your region revealed (fog of war), icons for fast travel; one reviewer notes mobile-style UI scattered in menus. Lesson: darkness/unrevealed areas as the border; cheap, no frame at all. Sources: [Mein-MMO map](https://mein-mmo.de/en/where-winds-meet-interactive-map-fast-travel-points-world-bosses-outposts-and-quests-at-a-glance,1537064/), [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/how-netease-built-an-authentic-wuxia-open-world-in-where-winds-meet).
7. **Romance of the Three Kingdoms VIII Remake / XIV** [src, partial]. Ink-painted scenic art with 2D layers and 3D depth; territory shown in faction colours (XIV). Lesson: colour washes over the painted map communicate state; the ink look is the art, not a border. Sources: [Koei Tecmo RTK XIV creators](https://koeitecmoeurope.com/rtk14/creator.html), [Worth Playing](https://worthplaying.com/article/2019/12/19/news/117470-romance-of-the-three-kingdoms-xiv-all-details-officer-customization-and-gameplay-elements-screens).
8. **Palace-sim mobile games (Zhen Huan Zhuan "宫斗养成 + 宫廷模拟经营", Gongting Ji)** [src, weak]. Store pages only say buildings + ranks + relationships run in parallel and that scenes are "realistic"; no description of the map frame found. Lesson: not verifiable here; eyeball the store screenshots. Sources: [App Store 甄嬛传](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/%E5%8F%AB%E6%88%91%E4%B8%87%E5%B2%81%E7%88%B7-%E7%94%84%E5%AC%9B%E4%BC%A0%E6%AD%A3%E7%89%88%E8%81%94%E5%8A%A8/id1272017016), [App Store 皇帝的一天 (Palace Museum app, interactive map of 30+ halls)](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/%E7%9A%87%E5%B8%9D%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%80%E5%A4%A9-%E6%95%85%E5%AE%AB%E5%87%BA%E5%93%81/id893942155?uo=4).
9. **Genshin Impact (Liyue region)** [src, weak]. Map organised in region tabs; praised for simplicity; Liyue designed for an "Eastern fantasy" atmosphere. Lesson: regional tabs/zoom levels instead of one overloaded frame. Sources: [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/mihoyo-launches-official-genshin-impact-interactive-map/), [Genshin wiki map tabs](https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/File:Floating_Abode_Map.png).
10. **Total War: Warhammer III parchment-UI mod discussions** [src, counter-evidence]. Players explicitly asked for parchment back. Lesson: parchment is liked by many players generally, but our owner has said no to parchment sheets twice; treat her taste, not the average, as the target. Source: [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3142209008/4521135854308796663).
11. **Classical Chinese painting practice of 留白 (liu bai, leaving blank) and mist-cropped 青绿山水** [mine]. A painted scene fades into empty silk/paper through brushed mist instead of a border; mist belts (云霞) separate near/far. Not verified by a linked source here.
12. **Forbidden City tourist / Palace Museum mini-program maps** [src, weak]. Interactive guided maps, utilitarian flat plans with pins. Lesson: pins and labels are the job; art frame is irrelevant there. Source: [Palace Museum mini-program page](https://mp-weixin-qq-com-443.webvpn.scuec.edu.cn/miniprogram/landing_page/YBIjracF-gK9t-1wtvy0zobY/en).

## Pattern across references
- The best examples have NO rectangular frame. The edge is made by the painting itself: ink wash (1, 5, 7), cloud/mist belts (2, 11), darkness/fog of war (6).
- Labels are small paper-like slips or plain type on the painting (3, 12), not heavy chips.
- The state of the world is shown by colour washes/markers over the art (7), tools live outside the picture area.

## 3 directions for OUR Dư đồ (kit cream UI; each has 1 mock in map-frame-research/)
**A. Mây vàng (gold-cloud edge, from refs 2 + 11).** Full-bleed painted map; the four edges dissolve into painted gold/white cloud-mist belts (transparent webp, a few variants tiled), no line, no border; mist can also sit between the east/west/main halls as dividers. Painted once, reused. Mock: A.
**B. Lưu bạch (ink-wash vignette, from refs 1, 5, 11).** The map fades out into the kit-cream page through a brushed ink-wash feathered edge (a painted alpha mask), like a painting on blank silk: no frame, no sheet, edge irregular but SOFT (the owner said "cut so it looks nice", not necessarily torn). Mock: B.
**C. Họa đồ chú thiêm (palace-plan drawing, from ref 3).** No frame; the map has a single hairline and hall names become small yellow paper slips (đề thiêm vàng) like the Qing plan drawings; a small title cartouche "Dư đồ". Needs a NEW label look = file in KIT_REQUESTS before any build. Mock: C (painted slip, placeholder placement).

## Open risk to confirm with the owner (cheap question)
She may be unhappy with the MAP PAINTING itself (tilted perspective, mixed palette, cropped at 2x zoom), not only its edge: "Dư đồ xấu quá". All three directions keep the painting. If she answers that the painting is the problem, the real task is a new bird's-eye "họa đồ" painting (flat Qing-plan / Qingming-scroll style), which is a bigger art job.

## Mocks (painted with gpt-2-6 AFTER the research; real CSS on the live Dư đồ, iPad 1180x820@2 mostly; files in map-frame-research/)
sheet-map-research.jpg = all three side by side; mock-{A,B,C}-{ipad,fold}.jpg singly (A has no Fold: needs its own 1:1 cloud painting).
- **A mây vàng**: painted gold/ivory cloud belts (overlay + silhouette mask, same technique as round 3). Reads as a Japanese screen / palace-in-clouds. Weakness: clouds are heavy and eat about 12% of the map on each side; on iPad the top/bottom belts cut labels (Cần Chính điện); they would need to be much thinner and lighter, and the map zoomed out a notch.
- **B lưu bạch**: ink-wash brush alpha mask on the map; page cream shows around. Softest and the most "painting", no border at all. Weakness: the mask also fades labels and tool buttons near the edge (I moved the tool row inward for the mock); on Fold the blob is small, so a 1:1-shaped brush mask is needed; the alpha mask webp is big (about 0.7-0.9 MB at full size, production must be 8-bit/lower res).
- **C họa đồ chú thiêm**: no frame at all (map full-bleed, 1px gold hairline), hall names on painted yellow paper slips. Weakness: slips are too saturated/large in this mock (kit chip look is replaced), which is a KIT change: would need KIT_REQUESTS before any build.
All three are rough mocks to judge DIRECTION, not final art. Mock assets: slip 600 px, cloud overlay 215 KB, mask 18 KB.

## My recommendation
B first (closest to refs 1, 5, 11 and the least chrome), A as the fallback if she wants some "palace" feel, C only if she likes yellow-slip labels (needs kit change). And ask her the one cheap question above (is it the frame or the map picture itself?) BEFORE any more painting.
