## 1. tt_oan .9 «Tam ty hội thẩm» — PLAUSIBLE (+ADJUST)
Real: Đường "tam ty suy sự" (Ngự sử đài + Hình bộ + Đại Lý tự) for grave cases; Ming/Qing "Tam pháp ty" = Hình bộ, Đô sát viện, Đại Lý tự
(Qing also "Cửu khanh hội thẩm"); Nguyễn kept "Tam pháp ty" under the same three names. Caution: in Lê/Nguyễn provincial government "Tam ty"
means Đô ty / Thừa ty / Hiến ty, and in Tống "Tam ty" was the FINANCE commission. So the bare phrase «Tam ty» is ambiguous to a reader who knows
history; the three-office meaning is right for a trial.
Drama: Chân Hoàn / Diên Hi: concubines never appear in the hall; the family's fate is argued by officials and decided by the Emperor; the
consort's only weapon is the bedside and the Thái hậu. The chapter's premise «Phi tần không được bước vào công đường. Mọi chứng cứ phải đi qua
tay người khác» is exactly this and is correct (the rule 後宮不得干政 is a founder's instruction in Ming and a Qing house law).
ADJUST (exact):
1. Name the offices once in the chapter goal `g:` so the player sees which three: «Án của {dad} được giao ba nha môn cùng xét: Hình bộ, Ngự sử
   đài, Đại Lý tự.» Keep the chapter title «Tam ty hội thẩm» (Đường usage) — do not change the id/title.
2. c1 «Một trong ba quan hội thẩm là thông gia nhà {v}» → this is a real institution: hồi tị (迴避). Reword the option «Xin đổi quan» →
   «Xin quan ấy hồi tị». Historically a related judge MUST recuse, so the penalty «bị nói là nghi ngờ triều đình» is too harsh; the cost should be
   that SHE (a consort) is raising a court matter: lx → «phi tần bàn việc quan».
3. x1 «Xin Hoàng thượng đến nghe» (Emperor attends the hearing) is not how it worked: the Emperor does not sit in a Tam pháp ty hearing; he
   confirms the verdict afterwards (Ming "Triều thẩm / Thu thẩm", Qing "Thu thẩm", Nguyễn "vua phê án"). Reword to «Xin Hoàng thượng ban
   dụ cho hội thẩm xét kỹ» with the rt «Hoàng thượng ban khẩu dụ: xét cho công bằng. Không ai dám bẻ cong khi dụ của vua còn đó.» Failure line
   already says the Emperor refuses ("phi tần không nên bàn án") — keep, it is the historical default.
Other checks in the chapter: s2 «Nhờ thái giám giữ ấn đưa vào» — eunuch passing outside matters was a capital-grade offence (Ming Hồng Vũ
iron tablet against eunuchs in politics), the existing "phạm lệ cung / Thái giám bị bắt" loss is right. «Viết tấu dâng thẳng» (consort
memorialising a case) is rare and offensive in history — the loss «phi tần dâng tấu án quan» is right, keep it as the risky road. Forgery
by "mực còn mới" is genre-correct (Tống "Tẩy oan tập lục" tradition of reading evidence). Ngự sử as a messenger: fine, but he must not be one
of the three trial officers — add «Ngự sử ngoài hội thẩm» in the option text to avoid a conflict of interest. Mother kneeling to the Emperor
(tt_oan .10 c2) is the standard "đệ đơn / khấu cung" scene — PLAUSIBLE.

## 2. tt_co .10 «Đêm trước triều hội» — the consort behind the screen — WRONG as written

rèm như hỏi: con gái ông có biết không». (tt_oan .10 x1 also says «Đứng sau rèm, chờ» for the verdict day — same fault.)
Real: 垂簾聽政 (thùy liêm thính chính; Vietnamese "buông rèm nhiếp chính") is a REGENCY: an empress dowager sits behind the curtain beside a

to a Thái hậu either once the Emperor rules in person. A consort in the court hall is the textbook violation of "hậu cung bất đắc can chính";
Qing Shunzhi's iron tablet and Ming's ancestral rule exist to forbid exactly that.
Drama: in Chân Hoàn / Diên Hi / Như Ý the consort learns the court through the pillow, the study room, the Thái hậu's palace and eunuchs; she
is punished the moment she is seen "can chính". (Hoa Phi mài mực in the study is the accepted soft form.)

- lose E line → «nhìn về phía ngươi như hỏi: con gái ông có biết không.»

## 3. tt_mat rare «Hoàng hậu tiến cử ngươi làm người kế vị Trung cung» (RD97L tt_mat_11) — ADJUST
Real: the Emperor (with the Thái hậu) chooses the empress; a living empress does not "nominate" a successor. What did happen: a dying or
ailing empress recommends a kinswoman, or an empress dowager installs her niece (Lữ hậu → Trương Yển, Từ Hy → Long Dụ — both sure). A
recommendation made to the Emperor and remembered is plausible; an appointment is not. Also a secret pact between an empress and a minister
(father) is "kết bè" (朋黨) — a grave charge — so the Emperor's laugh «Hai nàng giấu trẫm lâu thế» only works as a RARE act of grace.
Drama: dying empresses leaving a last request is a staple (Phú Sát hoàng hậu type scenes, recall); nobody has a healthy empress hand over the seat.
ADJUST (exact):
- tag «Hoàng hậu tiến cử ngươi làm người kế vị Trung cung» → «Hoàng hậu tiến lời, Hoàng thượng ghi nhớ». ti «Người Trung cung tiến cử» → «Người được Trung cung tiến lời».
- Text: keep «nếu một ngày nàng không còn, người nên ngồi ghế ấy là ngươi» but add the reason before it: «Hoàng hậu bệnh đã lâu, ho khan suốt mùa đông…» and let the Emperor answer «Trẫm nhớ lời nàng». The Emperor decides at ch13 «Tranh ngôi Trung cung».
- Rule (engine/BAL, not text): gate the beat on the Hoàng hậu being ill or childless (a Hoàng hậu with a living son prefers her own clan); reward = a flag/Lợi thế for ch13 («Trung cung tiến lời») + hh bond, NOT an automatic win of the seat (keeps OD:L1250 "rank earned").
- Optional: the pact being "xóa trước mặt vua" should read as the Hoàng hậu offering the pact up as proof of loyalty — fine.

## 4. tt_co .8 sealed memorial for 3 days (x1) — PLAUSIBLE as drama, ADJUST wording
Real: confidential memorials sealed in a locked box and read only by the Emperor is a Kangxi–Yongzheng practice (奏摺密折). An Emperor
handing one to a consort would be out of character for a real ruler; as a LOYALTY TEST it is a well-worn trope (the consort is left alone with
memorials/papers and watched; mài mực in the study; Hoa Phi's slip is a pillar scene of Chân Hoàn). The dread «niêm bị động → ngài đề phòng
ngươi một mùa» is exactly how those scenes end.
ADJUST: (a) «bản tấu mật» → «một hộp tấu niêm phong» (she holds the box, never the memorial); (b) give an in-world reason so it does not look
random: «Hoàng thượng sắp đi tế Giao, dặn: “Hộp này nàng trông giúp trẫm ba ngày. Chìa trẫm giữ.”» — a locked box with the key kept makes
«Mở xem, báo cha» a real act of breaking a seal (niêm), not just reading; (c) option «Xin Hoàng thượng đừng giao cho thiếp» is the HISTORICALLY WISEST
reply (a careful consort refuses) — make sure it is not the weakest in value (balance reviewer: it gives only e:"mid"; fine as a safe road).

## 5. tt_mat .7 «Hoàng hậu thử lòng» — lễ thân tằm — PLAUSIBLE
Real: 親蠶禮 — the empress's rite of silkworm (Hán–Đường–Tống–Thanh; Qing Tiên tàm đàn), paired with the Emperor's tịch điền / thân canh.
In the rite the empress leads the consorts in rank order, so "do well without outshining" and «lấn Trung cung» is exactly the court risk
(precedence in the procession). Drama: Qing palace dramas show the rite with the Hoàng hậu leading and the consorts picking mulberry leaves.
No change. Wording gloss: say «lễ Thân tằm» (not "nuôi tằm") so the rite is readable as the Hoàng hậu's duty.

## 6. dtq_vu .9 «Đối chất» at Đại Lý tự — PLAUSIBLE + ADJUST (from map line only)
Real: Đại Lý tự is the review court (re-examines Hình bộ's verdicts; Đường through Nguyễn). 對質 (đối chất) between accuser, accused and
witnesses is standard. Strong parallel: Nhạc Phi 1141–42 (forged "mạc tu hữu" charge, recalled by the "mười hai kim bài", Đại Lý tự officers
who pleaded for him were removed, recall) → the chapters .7 «Cha bị triệu về» + .9 are historically on target. Forged handwriting = a
recurring genre device.
ADJUST: (1) A consort's evidence reaches Đại Lý tự only through an official or family hand (the chapter already says "through a proper hand";
good) — name the hands: mẹ/huynh (gia thuộc đệ đơn), a thư lại who knows the father's brush, the father's staff officer; NOT her own eunuch.
(2) 謀反 (mưu phản) is among the Thập ác; real penalty is liên tọa (relatives exiled/killed, women into the palace's service). The game's
ending «condemned but spared (she keeps rank)» is a GAME-CANON softening (road endings never kill/lãnh cung); mark it in text as the
Emperor's grace: «Hoàng thượng nể tình, miễn tội liên đới cho ngươi», never as the normal law. (3) The Emperor does not appear at the Đại Lý
tự hearing; his judgement comes after (same as item 1).

## 7. dtq_binh .7 «Chén rượu thu binh quyền» — PLAUSIBLE
Real: 杯酒釋兵權 — Tống Thái Tổ, 961: after a banquet he persuaded Thạch Thủ Tín, Vương Thẩm Kỳ and other generals to give up command for
estates, wealth and marriage ties. The historicity of the exact scene is debated (it comes from later sources), which is fine for a drama.
Setup in the road (the Emperor wants dad's command back "without seeming ungrateful") is the same logic; Tống solved it with institutions:
Xu mật viện controls the call-up (điều binh), generals command only in the field (thống binh), and troops/commanders rotate (更戍法).
Hổ phù (.5) is correct as a two-half tally: one half with the Emperor, one with the commander; command needs the halves to match.
ADJUST: (1) She is not at the banquet — a ngự yến for generals has no consorts. She learns beforehand (Emperor drops a hint at night) or
afterwards (dad's letter / a eunuch). Option idea: «Báo cha uống ít, nói nhiều lời tạ ơn» (warn him = leak = risk). (2) The dad's reward
for yielding should be the Tống one: a high but empty title and wealth, children's marriages — not exile. That also gives the ending
«retires with honour» a real shape. (3) Keep the owner-facing name «Chén rượu thu binh quyền»; no need to say "Tống Thái Tổ" in the text.

## 8. muoi_lau .7 «Muối quan, muối lậu» — PLAUSIBLE
Real: salt monopoly since Hán Vũ Đế (Diêm thiết luận); Đường Lưu Yến reform; Ming "khai trung" (merchants carry grain to the border for a salt
voucher); Qing Lưỡng Hoài diêm vận sứ and licensed merchants; private salt (tư diêm) smuggling was endemic and the officials who ran the
monopoly were often the smugglers (Hoàng Sào and Vương Tiên Chi were salt smugglers — sure). Corruption inside the salt office is therefore
the most likely scandal for a salt family, and the title «muối quan / muối lậu» (官鹽/私鹽) is right Vietnamese.
Words to use: diêm dẫn (licence), Tuần diêm Ngự sử, Diêm vận sứ / Giám diêm, "dẫn" (quota). A boat "sổ thuyền" is a manifest — fine.
Origin note (not a chapter): merchants were the lowest class (sĩ nông công thương) and merchant daughters were rarely selected; an entry
ceiling of Đáp ứng for the salt family is historically right. Drama: Yangzhou salt merchants are the standard "rich but low" family.

## 9. td_an .10 «Tam ty xét lại» — PLAUSIBLE + ADJUST
Real: a reopened case (翻案/ 昭雪) needs the Emperor's order and usually a CHANGE OF POWER: Nhạc Phi was executed 1142 and cleared 1162 by
Tống Hiếu Tông; Nguyễn Trãi, Lệ Chi viên 1442, was cleared by Lê Thánh Tông in 1464. Both are 20-odd years later and under a new Emperor.
That fits our accession start (OD:L1066) and the sequence .8 proof → .9 dâng sớ → .10 retrial (edict first, hearing after) — good order.
ADJUST: (1) .10 should open with the Emperor's chỉ «chuẩn cho tam ty xét lại», not with the hearing; (2) {foed} (the accused's side) is
a powerful official: he must be shown recusing or being replaced (hồi tị), not sitting as judge; (3) use the same gloss as tt_oan .9
(«Hình bộ, Ngự sử đài, Đại Lý tự»); (4) the real timescale is decades — the game compresses it; no text change, only never write a year count.
Drama: rehabilitation after the heroine's rise is the standard payoff (Chân Hoàn: the heroine's family restored once she has power, recall).

## 10. thd_sach .9 «Văn tự ngục» — PLAUSIBLE + ADJUST
Real: Qing 文字獄 under Khang Hy / Ung Chính / Càn Long (Đới Danh Thế's Nam Sơn tập; the Tứ khố censorship); Ming Hồng Vũ (courtiers killed over
the wording of memorials). The closest SOFT precedent for our ending set is Tống: Ô đài thi án 1079 — Tô Thức jailed by the Ngự sử đài over lines of
poetry, then banished, not killed, after the Thái hoàng thái hậu's plea (recall). Positive precedent: authors presenting a work to the Emperor and
being honoured: Tư Mã Quang's Tư trị thông giám (title bestowed by Thần Tông), Lê Văn Hưu 1272, Ngô Sĩ Liên 1479 — so «dad honoured, book adopted»
is right too.
ADJUST: (1) the "seditious" charge must hang on ONE quoted phrase or a taboo name (húy), exactly how it worked — write the accuser's quote in the
card: «Ngự sử dẫn một câu trong sách…». (2) The accuser is a rival or an informant, not "the court". (3) «dad jailed (she keeps rank)»
softens the usual extended punishment (liên đới); mark it as the Emperor's grace, like item 6. (4) Book banning = «cấm lưu hành, thu ván
khắc» (in print culture they burned the woodblocks) — better flavour than "banned".

## RULES FOR THE ROAD WRITERS (B1, B2, B3) — apply from now, whole family
1. **A consort is never in a court hall.** Channels she may use (all historical): bên gối / Ngự thư phòng after court, Thái hậu, Hoàng hậu,
   family (mẹ, huynh) petitioning in their own name, a clean Ngự sử or official, a eunuch (with the palace-law risk), a note, a gift.
   Forbidden: sitting at triều hội, attending a trial, handling memorials as her own act, commanding eunuchs outside the palace.
2. **Verdicts and appointments belong to the Emperor** (he confirms Tam pháp ty verdicts, chooses Chancellors/Hoàng hậu with the Thái hậu).
   She can only influence, never decide.
3. **One gloss for the three courts**: Hình bộ, Ngự sử đài, Đại Lý tự — written once per chapter that says «Tam ty».
4. **Recusal (hồi tị)** is real and cheap: a judge related to a party withdraws; use it as an option, not as an insult to the court.
5. **Retrials need an Emperor's chỉ**; they follow a power change; show the chỉ first.
6. **Treason / sedition = liên đới**: game canon (no death, no lãnh cung at road ends) must appear as the Emperor's grace, not as normal law.
7. Keep Đại Hy mix explicit: Đường/Tống offices + Ming/Qing manners is OK; do not introduce a year, a real dynasty name or a real person in
   text (ROAD_MAP §5.2: no personal name for the Emperor).
