## B. PHÙ TANG — new road(s)
**Premise (real shape):** Japan (Yamato/Wa → Nihon; Hán-Việt «Phù Tang», «Oa», later «Nhật Bản») sent **kentōshi** (遣唐使 «khiển Đường sứ») to Tang: 20 missions, 630–838 [B/V]; route from Hakata, after 702 via the **Goto islands and straight across the sea to the Yangtze mouth** [V]; **about one third to one half of those who sailed never came back** [V sources differ]; **Abe no Nakamaro** stayed in China, shipwrecked trying to go home [V]; **Heguri no Hironari** drifted to Champa [V]; **Kūkai's ship 804 drifted to Fujian** [V].
