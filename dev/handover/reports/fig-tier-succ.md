## 1. History check (owner OD:L1327) — dress notes, game style (crossed-collar robes, no Qing feathered hat, no cheongsam collar)
- **Hoàng thái hậu (Empress Dowager)**: Han/Tang/Song court dress. Phoenix is the female-imperial motif (dragon is reserved for the sovereign), so: deep crimson / dark red with gold, phoenix embroidery on sleeves and skirt, peonies; phoenix crown (fengguan) with pearl strings and side ornaments; layered pearl necklaces, jade pendant/ruyi; richer and heavier than Hoàng hậu, dignified, hem at the feet. Colour by age: deep wine/crimson (a36), darker brown-red/gold for a51; a18/a26 stay brighter crimson.
- **Nữ đế (reigning Empress, Wu Zetian model)**: imperial yellow (Tang imperial colour) crossed-collar robe with gold dragons on chest/sleeves, sea-wave hem, jade belt; female-sovereign mianguan: flat board with hanging pearl strings plus phoenix ornaments (not a copy of a man's twelve-bead crown); no Qing hat, no cheongsam collar. She keeps phoenix ornaments in the crown (female sovereign) but wears dragons on the robe.
- Source basis: Tang/Song court-dress conventions (Wu Zetian ruled as Emperor 690-705, adopted imperial yellow and dragon/mian regalia; Song Empress Dowagers wore phoenix crowns and red/gold). Not re-verified online this session; art direction only.
