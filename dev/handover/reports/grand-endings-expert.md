## 1. Real history — how palace women's lives actually ended
Sources: Tư trị thông giám (Tư Mã Quang); Cựu/Tân Đường thư (Hậu phi truyện); Hán thư (Ngoại thích truyện); Sử ký (Lữ thái hậu bản kỷ); Thanh sử cảo (Hậu phi truyện); Minh sử (Hậu phi truyện); Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư (Ngô Sĩ Liên); Việt sử thông giám cương mục; Evelyn Rawski, *The Last Emperors* (1998, Qing palace system: tuyển tú, release of palace maids at 25); Keith McMahon, *Women Shall Not Rule* (2013) and *Celestial Women* (2016).

| Woman | Start | End shape | Lesson for the game |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Võ Tắc Thiên** (Wu Zetian, Tang) | Tài nhân of Thái Tông → nun at Cảm Nghiệp tự after his death | Hoàng hậu → Thiên hậu → **Hoàng đế** of Võ Chu (690); forced to abdicate (Thần Long chính biến 705); buried as Hoàng hậu under a **wordless stele** (Vô tự bi) | The one real *nữ đế* of China. Her road went THROUGH leaving the palace (convent) — "leave to rise" is real. History judges her both ways → good/evil empress variants are historical, not invented. |
| **Lữ hậu** (Lü Zhi, Hàn) | Wife of Lưu Bang | Thái hậu **nhiếp chính** 15 years; "người lợn" torture of Thích phu nhân; her clan wiped out right after her death (Chư Lã chi loạn) | The archetype of the **hated Thái hậu**: real power, real cruelty, legacy destroyed. |
| **Hiếu Trang** (Bố Mộc Bố Thái, Thanh) | Mongol princess married in for alliance | Thái hậu then **Thái hoàng thái hậu**, raised Khang Hy, steadied two successions; deeply **revered** | The archetype of the **revered Thái hậu**; also a foreign bride who became the dynasty's backbone (→ Bắc Địch / Nam Chiếu). |
| **Từ Hi** (Cixi, Thanh) | Lan quý nhân (low tú nữ) | Mother of the only son → Thái hậu, **buông rèm nhiếp chính** ~47 years; blamed for the dynasty's fall | Rise from low rank via a son; power held from *behind the curtain*, not the throne. |
| **Ỷ Lan** (Lý, Đại Việt) | Village girl (legend: leaning on a lan tree) | Nguyên phi, **regent while Lý Thánh Tông campaigned** (Champa 1069); Linh Nhân Thái hậu; loved for relief of the poor, but had Dương Thái hậu and 72 maids killed | Same woman = both "hiền" and "ác" acts. A grand ending can be judged on the *dominant* deeds. |
| **Dương Vân Nga** (Đinh–Tiền Lê) | Wife of Đinh Tiên Hoàng | Thái hậu who **handed the dragon robe to Lê Hoàn** (980) and became his Hoàng hậu — "Hoàng hậu hai triều" | "Kingmaker" ending: she does not rule, she chooses who rules. |
| **Lý Chiêu Hoàng** (Lý–Trần) | Princess made ruler at 6 | The only Vietnamese **nữ hoàng**; made to cede the throne to her husband Trần Cảnh (1225), later deposed as Hoàng hậu, remarried Lê Phụ Trần | A *puppet* empress ending — reaching the throne without power is a distinct (dark/bittersweet) shape. |
| **Huyền Trân công chúa** (Trần) | Princess | **Hòa thân** to Champa for Ô–Lý; escaped the widow's pyre, became a nun (Hương Tuyên) | Hòa thân → escape → religion: the "freedom through faith" shape. |
| **Ngọc Hân công chúa** (Lê–Tây Sơn) | Princess | Bắc cung Hoàng hậu of Quang Trung; widowed, wrote *Ai tư vãn* | "Love across enemy camps"; literary legacy ending. |
| **Vương Chiêu Quân** (Hàn) | Palace maid never summoned (painter bribe story) | **Volunteered** to marry the Hung Nô thiền vu; "Ninh Hồ Át thị"; peace on the border for decades | Cung nữ → foreign queen + peace for the realm. Choice to LEAVE unlocks the ending. |
| **Văn Thành công chúa** (Đường) | Clan princess | Bride of Tùng Tán Cán Bố (Tibet), brought Buddhism, crafts | Cultural-bridge ending. |
| **Ban Tiệp dư** (Hàn) | Virtuous consort | Withdrew to serve the Thái hậu at Trường Tín cung; poem *Đoàn phiến* | "Retreat with honour" — a quiet, dignified grand ending. |
| **Triệu Phi Yến** (Hàn) | Dancer | Hoàng hậu → after the emperor's death demoted to thứ nhân, suicide | Peak then fall: the *bad* reading of the same rise. |
| **Dương Quý phi** (Đường) | Wife of the emperor's son | Quý phi, "ba nghìn sủng ái"; strangled at Mã Ngôi (756) | "Ái phi họa quốc" — love + ruin; the *evil-love* variant in folk memory. |
| **Thượng Quan Uyển Nhi** (Đường) | Daughter of a condemned family, palace slave | **Nữ quan**, drafted edicts — "Cân quốc tể tướng"; killed in the 710 coup | Tội thần's daughter → power by talent, not by bed. Career ending for cung nữ / thầy đồ's daughter. |
| **Vạn Quý phi** (Minh) | Palace maid 17 years older than Thành Hóa | Unrivalled favour; accused of killing rivals' sons | Cung nữ + evil-favourite variant. |
| **Hiếu Hiền Thuần** (Phú Sát, Thanh) / **Kế hậu Ô Lạp Na Lạp** | Empresses of Càn Long | First loved and mourned; second **cut her hair** (1765), stripped of status, buried without honours | Two Hoàng hậu endings: beloved vs. erased (→ Như Ý truyện). |
| **Lệnh Ý hoàng quý phi** (Ngụy thị, Thanh) | Bao y (servant-class) family | Hoàng quý phi, mother of Gia Khánh, posthumous Hoàng hậu | Real base of Diên Hi: maid-class → empress-by-son. |
| **Hiếu Thánh Hiến** (Nữu Hỗ Lộc, Thanh) | Low-rank consort of Ung Chính | Mother of Càn Long, **Sùng Khánh Thái hậu**, lived to 86, honoured | Real base of Chân Hoàn's end. |
| **Từ Dũ** (Nguyễn) | Consort of Thiệu Trị | Mother of Tự Đức; Thái hậu famous for virtue ("Đức Từ Dũ"), the Từ Dũ hospital still carries her name | Virtue legacy that outlives the dynasty. |
| Minh **tuẫn táng** (to 1464) / Thanh **thái phi** rules | Consorts of a dead emperor | Buried with him (Minh until Anh Tông abolished it) / moved to the Thọ Khang cung or to their son's vương phủ (Thanh) | After the emperor dies the life *changes stage*; a son's phủ is a real "leave the palace" exit. |
| Thanh **cung nữ xuất cung** | Bao y maids from tuyển tú | Released at ~25, married outside; some stayed as ma ma / nữ quan | Ordinary maid exit is NORMAL; staying on to the top is the special road. |

**Real ending families** that recur: (1) Throne (nữ đế; puppet nữ hoàng); (2) Regency (Thái hậu buông rèm; kingmaker); (3) Empress (beloved / erased / deposed); (4) Mother of the heir (Thánh mẫu Thái hậu); (5) Favourite (sủng phi; yêu phi bị giết); (6) Office & talent (nữ quan, ngự y, văn chương); (7) Exit (xuất cung lấy chồng, xuất gia, hòa thân, theo con ra phủ); (8) Clan (minh oan, phục hưng gia tộc / diệt tộc); (9) Realm (hòa thân giữ biên cương, cứu dân); (10) Death-shaped (tuẫn táng, ban chết, lãnh cung) — the game keeps these as *lose* outcomes, never grand endings (j67 rule: never forced death / lãnh cung).

## 2. Palace dramas — how the stories the owner knows end
Sources: Hậu cung Chân Hoàn truyện (2011, Lưu Liễm Tử novel; dir. Trịnh Hiểu Long); Diên Hi công lược (2018, Vu Chính); Hậu cung Như Ý truyện (2018, Lưu Liễm Tử); Võ Tắc Thiên truyền kỳ (2014); Hiếu Trang bí sử (2003); Mị Nguyệt truyện (2015); Thâm cung nội chiến (TVB 2004); Đại Trường Kim (MBC 2003); Đồng Y (MBC 2010); Jang Ok Jung (SBS 2013); Thượng Quan Uyển Nhi (2016 Đại Đường nữ tướng / older 2001).

| Drama | Heroine's end | What the structure teaches |
|---|---|---|
| **Chân Hoàn truyện** | Naive Thường tại → loses child + love → *leaves* to Cam Lộ tự → love with Quả quận vương → returns as Hi phi with twins → brings down the Hoàng hậu → the emperor dies (her hand) → **Thánh mẫu Hoàng thái hậu**, alone. | The famous "won the palace, lost the heart" ending. Her destined road went *out* of the palace and back. NPC endings are each distinct (Hoa phi dies by her own hand against a wall, Thẩm Mi Trang dies in childbirth, An Lăng Dung bitter almonds, Hoàng hậu shut away "không chết không gặp", Ôn Thực Sơ). |
| **Diên Hi công lược** | Cung nữ Ngụy Anh Lạc enters to **avenge her sister** → revenge done → Lệnh quý phi, the emperor's love; friendship with Phú Sát hoàng hậu is the emotional core. | Revenge first, love second, friendship as the heart. Cung nữ → consort road is its spine. |
| **Như Ý truyện** | Childhood love with the emperor → Kế hậu → love rots → she **cuts her hair**, dies forgotten. | A Hoàng hậu ending that is *tragic by choice* (she refuses to keep fighting). Friendship with Hải Lan carries the show. |
| **Võ Tắc Thiên truyền kỳ** | Tài nhân → convent → Hoàng hậu → Hoàng đế. | Pure power road; the convent exit is required. |
| **Hiếu Trang bí sử** | Gives up her love (Đa Nhĩ Cổn) for the dynasty → revered Thái hoàng thái hậu. | Love sacrificed for power/realm = a *valid* grand ending (the destined ending may be the opposite of what the heart wants). |
| **Mị Nguyệt truyện** | Low concubine → hostage in Yên → **Tuyên Thái hậu**, regent of Tần. | Foreign exile + return to rule. |
| **Thâm cung nội chiến** | Ngọc Doanh (scheming) vs Nhị Hỷ (kind cung nữ) → the kind maid marries out, the schemer dies. | Same palace, opposite destinies by temperament. |
| **Đại Trường Kim** | Kitchen maid → first royal **female physician** (Đại Trường Kim); leaves with the man she loves. | Career + freedom ending for a cung nữ; talent not favour. |
| **Đồng Y** | Water-carrier maid → Thục tần, mother of King Anh Tổ. | Low maid → mother of a king, virtuous variant. |
| **Jang Ok Jung** | Court lady → **Hi tần → Vương phi → deposed → poisoned**. | The most famous *villainess* ending in Korea; evil roads end high, then pay. |

**Drama pattern**: each heroine has ONE destined arc that the audience feels only in hindsight; the turning point is almost always a choice the viewer would not have made "safely" (leave the palace, give up a love, use poison, refuse to fight). Side characters have equally fixed destinies → supports NPC parity.

## 3. Games — branching destined endings, carried titles, big rewards
Sources: Princess Maker 2 (Gainax 1993; ~70 endings); Long Live the Queen (Hanako Games 2012); Reigns: Her Majesty (Nerial 2017); Crusader Kings III (Paradox 2020); Mystic Messenger (Cheritz 2016); Code: Realize, Amnesia (Otomate); Orange Light (橙光) palace visual novels, e.g. 《深宫曲》 / 《后宫·甄嬛传》 fan VNs (ending galleries "kết cục đồ giám"); NieR:Automata (2017, endings A–Z, ending E); Undertale (2015, true-pacifist ending); Hades (2020, epilogue + Fated List); Rogue Legacy (2013); World of Warcraft / Black Desert / Lost Ark (titles shown above the name); Genshin Impact (namecards, achievement titles).

| Game | Ending structure | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| **Princess Maker 2** | Ending decided by her final stat profile + key events (Queen, General, Royal Consort, Dark Lord…); rare ones (Queen, "Bride of the Prince") need tight conditions; evil endings are equal citizens. | Stat-profile + key choice gating. Evil endings allowed and loved. |
| **Long Live the Queen** | Many endings (most are deaths); the "good" crowning has variants (beloved / feared). | Same throne, different *kind* of ruler. |
| **Reigns: Her Majesty** | Each reign ends; a few "objectives" are hidden and discovered by acting. | Hidden goals revealed only by doing. |
| **Crusader Kings III** | Life continues after milestones; legacy carries between heirs. | Milestone ≠ game over; life goes on. |
| **Mystic Messenger / otome** | Per route: **Good / Normal / Bad** endings + rare "true" ending; ending gallery (CG) collected across plays. | A fixed per-route count (e.g. 1 true + 2 good + normal + bad) — clear and collectable. |
| **Orange Light palace VNs** | 10–30 endings per story (Hoàng hậu / Thái hậu / xuất cung / lãnh cung / nữ đế…), each with its own CG and a named "kết cục"; players share ending lists. | Exactly the owner's genre: named endings + one picture each + collection pride. |
| **NieR:Automata / Undertale** | The "true" ending needs choices the player resists (sacrifice, mercy). | The destined ending may demand the *uncomfortable* choice. |
| **Hades / Rogue Legacy** | Runs end; a meta list ("Fated List") remembers what was achieved. | Remember everything, carry little. |
| **MMO titles** (WoW, Black Desert, Lost Ark, CN mobile 称号) | Titles worn ABOVE the name; legendary titles glow/animate; rarest titles are the status symbol, shown on leaderboards. | The *carried title* as pure prestige — exactly the owner's "đeo lên trên đầu". Rarity is the reward. |

**What a "very large reward" looks like** (in-life): a jump of rank/office (CK3 titles, PM2 Queen), a unique treasure that cannot be sold, a permanent new place/role in the world (her own palace, a regency, a vương phủ), a big one-off grant of silver/goods, every NPC's attitude shifting (fame), and a unique CG/scene. Across lives: only prestige (title, gallery, leaderboard) — matches the owner's rule (title is the ONLY carry-over).

**What a carried title looks like elsewhere**: short, proud, 2–4 words ("Kingslayer", "Thiên hậu"), a tier colour + sparkle for the rarest, a tooltip "how earned", shown on leaderboards and to every other player. Game-to-game: never a stat that breaks the new run (Hades/Rogue Legacy keep power gains small; MMOs keep title stats modest). → our rule OD 13:50Z–13:55Z (one worn title, one modest bonus) already fits; the grand-ending title should follow the same rule (owner decides whether it carries a bonus into the next life — see design §open questions).

## 5. Rules the history/drama/game evidence supports (feed into the design)
1. **One destined ending per life, hidden**: dramas reveal destiny only in hindsight; Reigns-style hidden objectives. ✔ owner rule.
2. **The unlocking choice is often the uncomfortable one** (leave the palace, give up love, be cruel): Võ Tắc Thiên, Chân Hoàn, Hiếu Trang, NieR/Undertale. ✔ owner's examples (stay vs leave vs follow a Vương gia; evil empress).
3. **Same summit, different kind**: hiền vs bạo empress, revered vs hated Thái hậu — real (Hiếu Trang vs Lữ hậu) and in games (Long Live the Queen). ✔ variety guard.
4. **Milestone, then life goes on**: CK3 / real lives (Thái hậu lived decades after). Supports "event + scene + reward, life continues".
5. **Prestige is what carries**: MMO titles, galleries, Fated List; never power that breaks the next life. ✔ title is the only carry-over.
6. **Death / lãnh cung are lose outcomes, not grand endings** (matches j67 law "never forced death / lãnh cung / mưu phản").
7. **Fixed per-route counts make it collectable** (otome: true/good/normal/bad; Orange Light named endings). ✔ owner wants fixed counts.
