## 2. What the court actually gave (分例, "phần lệ")

### 2.1 Structure (this part is solid [A])
Allowances were fixed **by rank**, paid and issued by the household office, in four kinds: (1) **silver** (a periodic stipend), (2) **grain, meat, vegetables, tea, oil, milk** (a daily/monthly food ration), (3) **fuel and light** (charcoal, firewood, candles/oil for winter and night), (4) **cloth** (bolts of silk/satin at fixed intervals, plus festival issues of clothing), plus a **servant quota** (the number of maids and eunuchs the rank was allowed — and each had to be paid and fed too).
Higher rank = more of everything AND finer grade of the same thing. The grade difference mattered as much as the quantity (see 2.2).

### 2.2 Qing figures [B]
Commonly cited Qianlong-era yearly silver (taels, 兩): **Hoàng hậu ≈ 1000, Hoàng quý phi ≈ 800, Quý phi ≈ 600, Phi ≈ 400, Tần ≈ 300, Quý nhân ≈ 200, Thường tại ≈ 100, Đáp ứng ≈ 50–80**. Different editions give different values; some sources list them per month or add reign-specific increases. Use only the **ratio**: top ≈ 20× bottom.
Servants (commonly cited, [B]): Hoàng hậu ≈ 10 maids and a larger body of eunuchs; Hoàng quý phi/Quý phi ≈ 8; Phi/Tần ≈ 6; Quý nhân ≈ 4; Thường tại ≈ 3; Đáp ứng ≈ 2 (eunuch counts similar in shape). **The key game-relevant fact: each rank's silver had to feed its own retinue, and the retinue counts rose with rank** — so real "take-home" grew far less than the headline figure. (This is exactly the owner's balance law C.)
Food: a rank-scaled daily ration of pork (heavier for higher ranks), also mutton/poultry/fish on higher tables, vegetables, rice, **tea by named grade** (the finest teas such as Longjing, Biluochun, Pu'er went only to the top), **milk from rank-allotted cows** (the Empress had the largest herd) [B]. Fuel: the Empress and the highest consorts burned the better smokeless charcoal (the grade names in the item list — "silver frost", "red gauze" — are real, [B]); lower ranks burned coarse charcoal/firewood, in fixed monthly amounts for the cold months only [B]. Light: candles for high ranks, oil lamps for low ones [B].
Cloth: fixed yearly bolts by rank, plus festival clothing issued at Spring Festival / Dragon Boat / Mid-Autumn / winter solstice; the **fabric grade and colour were governed by sumptuary law** (imperial yellow for the Emperor, bright yellow for the Empress, etc.) [A].

### 2.3 Ming figures [B]
Ming palace women lived on **grain (禄米/月米) and silver (月例/月錢)**, the stipend scaled by office/rank (empress ≫ consorts ≫ palace officials ≫ ordinary maids). Insider accounts (《酌中志》) describe daily food prepared by the 尚食 office and festival issues; I recall no clean per-rank table that I could trust [?]. Ming is mainly valuable for **how goods moved**: orders through the Lục thượng/eunuch offices, with the eunuch supervisors (司禮監, 內官監) controlling the real supply — which opened the door to skimming (2.5).

### 2.4 Cosmetics specifically
- **What was used** [A for the kinds; B for the grades]: **rice powder / lead-white powder / pearl powder** (面粉, 鉛粉, 珍珠粉); **rouge** (胭脂, from safflower and later rose, in cakes, creams and rouge-impregnated paper); **lip colour** (口脂/唇紙, rouge plus wax or oil; cinnabar colour in older recipes); **brow colour** (黛: 青黛 blue-grey; 螺子黛 loa tử đại from Persia/Central Asia, made into small snail-shaped cakes; and in Ming–Qing **willow-charcoal** 柳炭 sticks as the cheap drawing brow stick); **hair oil** (桂花油 osmanthus oil, 刨花水 the mucilage of soaked elm-wood shavings); **scented waters and sachets**; **face creams** (面脂); **forehead ornaments** (花鈿). The Qing court medical records preserve face-powder and cream recipes (e.g. 玉容散 "jade-face powder") [A].
- **Were cosmetics in the monthly ration?** Qing consorts and ladies certainly drew **hair oil, powder, rouge** through the household office; they appear in **festival and gift ledgers** (rouge and powder were standard gifts to ladies) [B]. A precise "X boxes of rouge per rank per month" I cannot give [?]. What I can say with confidence is: (1) there was a **cosmetic and hair-oil line** within the allowance, not a separate system; (2) the **grade of the goods** rose with rank; (3) **maids bought extras from their own stipend and from tips** (《宮女談往錄》: a maid's pay was small, a few taels a month or less, and rouge/powder came out of it or out of gifts) [B].
- **Fiction as a model of the "powder money" idea**: in 《紅樓夢》 each young mistress drew a fixed monthly sum for hair oil and powder (「頭油脂粉」), served as a standing allowance and quarrelled over (the rose-nitre vs jasmine-powder row of chapters 59–60, with maids, concubines, borrowed and substituted cosmetics, and a mistress's punishment) [F, strong: closest ready-made template for "borrow / swap / steal cosmetics" drama].
- **Tribute and imperial bestowal.** Rare cosmetics came as **tribute (貢品)** from provinces and vassal states and went to the Emperor, who **bestowed (賞賜)** them as favour: the standard bestowal list at festivals, births, promotions and for good service contained silk, jewels, tea, **rouge and powder**, perfumes, medicines [A]. The **loa tử đại** is the classic: a Persian blue-green brow-ink, in the Sui court a single piece reputedly worth ten gold, given by Emperor Yang to a favoured palace woman [B, an anecdote from late-Sui/Tang writing]; its fame outlived its use, and later courts used it as a symbol of favour. Other real tribute pieces: **kỳ nam / agarwood** from Đại Việt and Champa, **long diên hương / ambergris**, Eastern pearls (東珠) which in Qing sumptuary rules were rank markers (the Empress's crown held more; a pearl as a bestowal was a statement) [A for the rank markers; B for the specific tribute routes].

### 2.5 How things ran out, and what people did
- **Ran out early**: allowances were fixed and the household office was both a supplier and a self-serving bureaucracy. Eunuch/office officials **skimmed** ("克扣份例" — withholding or substituting a lower grade); a consort without a **门包 (door-fee/tip)** to the supervising eunuch got the worst, the late, or the short ration. This is documented across the Qing and is the backbone of palace-drama plots [A that skimming was endemic; B on forms].
- **Buying extra**: through eunuchs/maids who went out of the palace with passes (officially restricted), or through **chartered court merchants/purchasing officials**; the price was the market price plus a fee; wealthy consorts could pay, low ranks could not. In 《宮女談往錄》 maids saved for years [B].
- **Borrowing / begging / gifts**: **sharing among friends and household** (a standard social currency); **gifts upward** to patrons (the Empress/Dowager) to gain protection; **bestowals** downward. In fiction (《甄嬛傳》) the Emperor's gift of an item to a favoured consort can itself be the weapon.
- **Stealing / smuggling / selling out**: attested in ledgers and court cases for eunuchs and maids; **stealing imperial property (盜內府財物)** was heavily punished in law (a capital charge for serious cases in the statutes) [A on severity; B on the exact sentence]. A palace maid caught stealing from a mistress: beating by the household court (慎刑司, "杖" with rods), dismissal from rank, banishment to heavy labour (the Ming **浣衣局**, the Qing **辛者庫** or exile to the frontier), and for repeated or high-value theft death [B].
- **Cosmetics as poison.**
  - *History*: the commonest real danger was **chronic**: lead-white powder, mercury-based "water-silver powder", and cinnabar lip colour harmed skin and health; this is attested by medicine, archaeology and the cosmetic literature [A]. Well-documented single court cases of **deliberately poisoned rouge** are not something I can name with confidence [?]; the better-attested poisoning vehicles at court were **food, drink, medicine and incense/perfume** (Han Empress Xu's poisoning through a medicinal draft is the standard example) [A].
  - *Dramas [F]*: 《甄嬛傳》: **歡宜香** (Huanyi incense with musk) given by the Emperor to the favourite consort Hua Fei, who is infertile as a result — the poison arrives *as a bestowal*; **舒痕膠** (a scar-removing salve with musk) leads to a lost pregnancy; also incense, sachets and food used as vehicles. 《延禧攻略》: a musk-laced sachet is detected by the heroine; low-rank maids use **handmade velvet flowers (絨花)** as modest, personal gifts. 《如懿傳》: gifted sweets and medicines; also the humiliation of a consort's allowances being cut. (Details beyond the Huanyi incense and the scar salve: [?]; check before quoting.)
  - **Allergy** (the owner's word): maps to a reaction (rash, swelling), not death: "bột ngứa" already exists in the game as a similar item.

## 4. What the game could add (all PARKED: feature freeze OD:L1402, build only after current work)
Keyed to the owner's 08:25Z answers (OD:L1577). Each proposal: **Gain**, **Risk** (likelihood / severity / how to detect or fix) and one **Verdict** line. What exists is marked [exists].

### P1. Ration by rank, issued monthly by Nội vụ phủ
Rank group → box tier and count (see item list: L=T1, M=T1/T2, H=T2/T3, E=T3). Low ranks get little, and that is accepted (owner). A **monthly theme** (history: some months brow, some son, some rouge, per owner 08:15Z) — one "full box" of the theme item plus a small share of the other parts.
- **Gain**: gives the player something to plan and talk about every month; gives the existing makeup system a reason to be limited; uses already-existing Nội vụ phủ issue code (outfit/veil issue [exists in jearn]).
- **Risk**: players feel "the game is stingy" at low rank — likelihood **may happen**, severity **medium**; detect: playtest + bot "makeup days per month by rank"; fix: raise the box (`BAL.toil.box`) or the issue count, one knob.
- **Risk**: more bookkeeping (another monthly card) — likelihood **likely**, severity **small**; fix: fold into the existing monthly news card (kit part, no new UI).
- **Verdict**: Gain bigger.

### P2. Palette slots usable only when owned
Makeup colours/parts are limited to what is in the box: no rouge item → no má hồng part; no brow ink → draw with charcoal, or bare. Existing levels already reward fewer parts via lower result (Mặt mộc / Vụng về); a missing part simply caps the level.
- **Gain**: matches the owner's picture exactly; makes the item list matter; ties to the existing kit/makeup scene.
- **Risk**: breaking the **existing makeup flow and contest scoring** (jmkt tsMkPts, toilAllow) — likelihood **likely if done carelessly**, severity **big** (contest results shift); detect: dev/test/makeup-theme.js + `dev/measure/makeup-theme-bot.js` before/after; fix: ship behind a BAL switch off by default.
- **Risk**: old saves have no box — likelihood **certain**, severity **small**; fix: grant a starting box on load (as jearn does for outfits).
- **Verdict**: Gain bigger, as long as it ships with the guard test and a BAL switch.

### P3. Items run out — the usage math (OD:L1577 D)
Owner's rule: ONE makeup per day; each result spends lượt from each item it uses: Mặt mộc 0, Vụng về/Đoan trang 1, Kiều diễm/Khuynh thành 2, Diêm dúa 3; a box = `BAL.toil.box` (start 20) per item; light or skipped days leave more for festivals.
**Worked numbers (30-day month, one makeup a day):**
| Daily look | lượt per item per day | per 30 days | boxes (20) per item per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| all Đoan trang | 1 | 30 | 1.5 |
| all Kiều diễm | 2 | 60 | 3 |
| all Diêm dúa | 3 | 90 | 4.5 |
| mix: 20 plain (1), 8 fancy (2), 2 festival-big (3) | — | 20+16+6 = 42 | 2.1 |
So a **single box per month covers only about 20 plain days**: that matches "item runs out" and forces the choice "plain every day vs fancy on festivals". Design target (for Foreman D to measure): the ration is about **two thirds of the expected need at that rank** (higher ranks have more festival/occasion days where `toilAllow` demands Kiều diễm+, so their need is higher and their ration proportionally higher): this is the balance law in numbers — slack is the same fraction at each rank. If a part runs out mid-month the player does makeup without it (level capped), borrows, begs or buys (P4–P6).
- **Gain**: a real, measurable consumable with one knob (`BAL.toil.box`) and the owner's own table.
- **Risk**: pressure too high so the player quits makeup altogether — likelihood **may happen**, severity **medium**; detect: bot "makeup days / month" by rank (target ≈ two thirds of days made up at any rank); fix: `BAL.toil.box` +5.
- **Risk**: pressure too low (one box is plenty) so nothing is felt — likelihood **may happen**, severity **small**; same measure, knob −5.
- **Verdict**: Gain bigger.

### P4. Borrow from NPCs (by lender's trait, mood, relation)
Ask a consort/maid/friend for a measure of an item. Success from thiện cảm, mood (the emotion bar), trait (Hào phóng/Keo kiệt…), and whether they have spare; a refusal costs a little thiện cảm; a yes creates a **small debt** (a favour owed; the lender may later ask in return). Two-way: NPCs ask her too, and her answer is remembered (j24-style visits).
- **Gain**: ties makeup scarcity to the relationship web; reuses relation/mood code; NPCs behave like her (owner: "NPC thì tất nhiên cũng phải lo đồ như tôi").
- **Risk**: new "ask for item" action overlaps the existing gift/request flows (`giveTo`, "xin" Hoàng hậu/Thái hậu in jveil) — likelihood **likely**, severity **medium**; fix: reuse jveil's "xin" function; a **LUẬT KIT TRƯỚC** check for any new row.
- **Risk**: friend-spam (asking every day) — likelihood **likely**, severity **small**; detect: bot count; fix: a cooldown per lender (as in the veil friend gift 12%/month).
- **Verdict**: Gain bigger.

### P5. Beg (xin) from Hoàng hậu / Thái hậu / household officers
The "xin" road that jveil already has (odds 15 + hh/2 and 10 + th/2): extend from the veil to cosmetics. Fail costs favour.
- **Gain**: nearly free to build (function exists). **Risk**: players beg every month — likelihood **likely**, severity **small**; fix: odds fall with repeats. **Verdict**: Gain bigger.

### P6. Rush-buy (mua gấp) with silver
At a price premium, through (a) Nội vụ phủ (full price, slow), (b) the passing hàng rong (existing, 6 items per turn), (c) **lén mua ngoài cung** via a loyal ma ma/eunuch/maid (a price discount but a 12% leak that costs Danh −3, as in jveil). Skimming (2.5): paying a **tip (lệ tiền)** to the supervising eunuch makes the monthly ration come in full; refusing risks a short issue.
- **Gain**: gives silver a daily use that keeps the economy honest at high rank (the player who is "rich" still pays: balance law C). **Risk**: silver inflation at high rank makes it moot — likelihood **may happen**, severity **medium**; detect: Foreman D's per-rank silver slack; fix: premium price scaled by rank. **Verdict**: Gain bigger.

### P7. Steal (two-way) with consequences
Steal from a rival's box (needs a loyal servant or access to the room; uses the existing Soát rương/Khám cung machinery in reverse). Caught → Danh and Đức loss, the **household court** beating and cut in ration (history 2.5), a feud with the victim. NPCs steal from her out of **jealousy after a bestowal** (the existing `bestow()` adds ghen +3 to others — now it can fire a theft).
- **Gain**: rich drama (Hồng lâu mộng ch.59–60 pattern) from one new verb. **Risk**: the "steal" verb becomes the dominant cheap road — likelihood **likely**, severity **big** (breaks the equivalence rule OD 15/11 20:30Z: every bạc road has an equal-value alternative); detect: bot choice share; fix: catch odds rise with rank and with the victim's Tai mắt; tune so stealing is the hesitation case, not the obvious pick. **Risk**: NPC theft makes the player's life feel unfair — likelihood **may happen**, severity **medium**; fix: she always gets a clue (a missing-item notice, never an invisible loss). **Verdict**: Gain bigger, if the catch odds are measured first.

### P8. Poison through the existing harm acts
Cosmetics join the TAINT list: a taint turns a box item into a vehicle for **allergy** (rash, Sắc penalty until healed; the existing `bột ngứa` and `hủy dung` systems are the template), or the heavier existing acts. No new harm, only a new vehicle (`h` hidden, `t` tainted, `by` — all exist on every inventory entry).
- **Gain**: the owner's idea on top of working code. **Risk**: a **hủy dung** chain from cosmetic + scar + veil becomes too frequent — likelihood **may happen**, severity **big**; detect: bot "faces scarred per life"; fix: allergy gives a mild status only (Sắc −N for a few weeks), keep hủy dung for the existing heavy act.
- **Risk**: players avoid ALL gifts of cosmetics (paranoia) — likelihood **likely**, severity **small** (it IS the drama); fix: an Tai mắt/physician check road already exists (Khám).
- **Verdict**: Gain bigger.

### P9. Emperor bestows rare tribute cosmetics (loa tử đại etc.)
Add the item list's T4/Ngự tứ pieces to `bestow(why)` for special occasions (sinh thần, thăng phẩm, long favour); each such bestowal also fires the existing jealousy (+3 ghen). Wearing it in public marks her (Vượt phẩm accusations exist when the item outranks her).
- **Gain**: the "vinh hạnh" moment the owner described; art-ready. **Risk**: rare drops are never seen — likelihood **may happen**, severity **small**; fix: tie a guaranteed one to a milestone. **Verdict**: Gain bigger.

### P10. BALANCE LAW (C): high ranks feel as tight as low ranks — NOT parked: a check now
History supports it directly: allowances rose ~20× from Đáp ứng to Hoàng hậu, but retinue (10 vs 2), required festival display, gifts expected upward and downward, and scrutiny rose too. The game already has levers for that: servant caps by rank (`BAL.promo.cap`), Vượt phẩm accusations, jealousy from bestowals, and `toilAllow` (festival makeup demanded of higher ranks).
**Suggested costs that grow with rank** (proposals; each needs a number from the bot):
1. **Retinue upkeep**: each servant draws part of the ration (so a Phi with 6 servants feeds 6).
2. **Expected gifts**: festival gifts upward and to rivals/friends rise with rank (thiện cảm penalty if skipped).
3. **Display duty**: big occasions require Kiều diễm+ (already in jmkt: `toilAllow` shifts), which costs 2 lượt per item.
4. **Skimming and tips**: the higher the rank, the more people (eunuch, ma ma) with a hand out.
5. **Scrutiny**: Vượt phẩm accusations and a rival's Khám cung scale with Sủng.
- **Gain**: the owner's explicit rule; fits history; prevents the "rich endgame, no pressure" collapse.
- **Risk**: overshoot (high ranks become impossible) — likelihood **may happen**, severity **big**; detect: **Foreman D** measure of silver/Sức/khắc slack per rank (target ≈ flat, owner OD:L1577 C), fix: scale each cost by a single BAL knob per rank group.
- **Risk**: undershoot (no one notices) — likelihood **may happen**, severity **medium**; same measure.
- **Verdict**: Gain bigger — and it is the one item that needs a measure BEFORE any ration code, so Foreman D should include it now.
