## (1) Qing practice
- Banner girls (八旗秀女) were reviewed every 3 years; Board of Revenue (户部) registered them; on review day they came in carts to the north gate (神武门) and were led in by eunuchs, lined up in rows, and viewed by the emperor / empress dowager / empress. [K](hi)
- 留牌子 = tag kept, i.e. NOT yet a consort; 撂牌子 = dismissed, free to marry. Kept girls were called back for a second look (复看), and only after that were they confirmed and ranked. [K](hi for the two-step; mid for exact gap length)
- Between the two looks the kept girls normally went HOME and were summoned again; I know of no standing dormitory for them in the Qing palace. They entered the palace to live only when assigned a rank (答应/常在/贵人…). [K](mid)
- 储秀宫 and its neighbours were ordinary consort residences (Cixi lived at 储秀宫) — not a girls' hostel. A freshly ranked low consort was often lodged as a "co-resident" in a side hall of a senior consort's palace. [K](mid)
- The girls who DID live inside and trained were the Nội vụ phủ bao-yi girls (内务府 官女子/宫女), taken at ~13, serving until ~25. [K](hi)
- Gap: 体元殿 as a tú nữ waiting hall — I cannot confirm; treat as [lo], do not use.

## (2) Ming practice
- For an empress / consort selection, chosen girls (淑女) were kept inside the palace for a period (months up to a year) and trained by female officials (女官, 宫正司 / 六尚 offices) in manners, speech, writing, before the final choice. [K](mid; details differ by reign)
- Quarters: shared halls/rows of small rooms in the inner court under female officials and eunuch guards; no named single "tú nữ palace" I can cite. [K](lo)
- Ming maids lived in rows of rooms (e.g. around 针工局, 浣衣局; sick ones sent to 安乐堂). [K](mid)

## (3) Dramas players know
- Common to 甄嬛传 / 如懿传 / 延禧攻略: after selection the new girls stay together in a side court or halls under a 教习嬷嬷 / senior 姑姑 who drills 规矩 (walk, bow, 请安, 福身), has a ruler, inspects body and dress, enforces curfew and lamps-out. [D](mid)
- Typical shown layout: girls share rooms of 2–4 on a heated 炕/bed, a screen between, a small maid attending several girls, one common hall (厅) where the ma ma lectures and girls meet. [D](mid)
- Typical drama beats there: tripped in the corridor, ruined dress, a tray swapped, someone reported to the ma ma, friendships of the first night (甄嬛–沈眉庄–安陵容 pairing). [D](mid; already seeded in HIST-TUYENTU)
- Maids (宫女) in the same shows: a back-row range (后罩房/倒座房) behind the main hall of a palace, long shared plank beds (大通铺) 6–8 to a room, a 管事姑姑 (head maid) controlling doors and rations; 延禧攻略 shows the sewing workshop (绣坊) as the maids' world. [D](mid) Real: same pattern, maids housed behind their mistress's palace. [K](mid)

### (1) Did tú nữ stay several days inside before/after selection?
- Qing, before: NO standing multi-day stay. Banner girls came on the appointed day by cart to 神武门, were led in and viewed the same day in rows. [K](mid) Waiting happened at the gate / in carts, not in a hostel. [K](mid-lo)
- Qing, after: 留牌子 girls went home and were recalled for 复看; rank came after, weeks later. [K](mid) So "wait for conferral inside the palace" is not Qing practice; it is a compression.
- Ming: chosen girls were kept INSIDE for months under 女官 for training before the final pick. [K](mid; varies by reign) This is the best real anchor for a multi-day stay inside.
- Qing detail worth knowing: Guangxu's 1889 empress selection was held in the 储秀宫 front hall 体和殿 with Cixi presiding. [K](mid-lo) (a reason to use 储秀宫 as the review place, but it was a consort palace, not a hostel.)
- Dramas (甄嬛传 / 延禧攻略): girls arrive together, stay several days in a shared court under a 教习嬷嬷, then the review day, then a wait for the edict. [D](mid) The owner's memory matches the DRAMA flow and Ming; it does not match plain Qing. Safe: never name a dynasty in text.

### (2) Who assigned rooms; usable incidents per day
- Assigner: 内务府 officials + 敬事房 eunuchs at the gate/yard; inside, a 教习嬷嬷 / 女官 (Ming 宫正司). [K](mid) Eunuchs stay at the gate and outer yard; girls' rooms are entered by the ma ma only. [K](mid)
- Order by family rank / banner / bribes: better families get the better room, bed by the window or away from the draught and the night-pail; bribing the ma ma for a better bed or for roommates. [D](mid)
- DAY 1 (enter, rooms): luggage/ornament check (plain dress, few jewels) · roll-call and seating by birth · the ma ma's first lecture on 规矩 · a bed swapped or a roommate who snores/weeps · a lost hairpin accused on the new girl · first night talk (homesick, share food/medicine) · a bribe to the eunuch · a girl falling ill and hiding it. [D](mid; sickness = delayed look [K](mid-lo))
