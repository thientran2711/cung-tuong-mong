## 1. Season dress (換季 — đổi y phục theo mùa)

| fact | detail | game use |
|---|---|---|
| The court changed dress on ANNOUNCED dates, not by weather | Lễ bộ / Nội vụ phủ named the day: summer dress (涼帽, gauze 紗 robes) from about the 3rd month, winter dress (暖帽, fur 皮) from about the 9th–10th month; spring/autumn = lined silk (夾), early winter = padded (棉), deep winter = fur in a fixed order (lighter fur → sable) | season of a robe = its FABRIC, read from the piece (sa / the = Hạ, lụa = Xuân·Hạ·Thu, gấm = Thu·Đông, lông chồn / hồ cừu = Đông, plain vải = all year) |
| Wearing the wrong season's dress was 失儀 (thất nghi, a breach of etiquette) | punished by reprimand, a fine of stipend, loss of face before Thái hậu / Hoàng hậu at the morning greeting; for the high-ranked it is worse (more eyes, model for the others) | off-season robe → Danh and the Thái hậu's goodwill drop, bigger at higher rank (law 5); the same rule for every consort (law 1) |
| Dramas: a consort in summer gauze in autumn is mocked as "không biết lễ" or "cố ý khoe dáng"; heavy brocade in summer = sweating, fainting, "phô trương" | the rich off-season piece still shows her beauty | law 3: the off-season piece keeps its own gain (Sắc / Danh of the piece), the cost is the etiquette loss |

Notes
- Rank colours (Qing): minh hoàng (bright yellow) for Hoàng hậu / Thái hậu; Hoàng quý phi / Quý phi golden yellow; Phi and Tần 香色 / ochre-gold; the low ranks have no prescribed colour. The game already guards rank through the piece's phẩm (j92 Vượt phẩm, jdress "Tố vượt phẩm"), so the dress code does NOT add a second rank-colour rule (one core, law 4). The painted t7/t8 outfits already carry the yellow (Foreman C t8-v2).
- Mourning white: quốc tang already forbids đỏ / vàng (j92 wdTang/wdFestive). The festival taboo list is the same idea at feast days (white / black = ill omen at a birthday or New Year).
- Lower consorts were watched less: in the dramas an Đáp ứng in a plain robe is ignored, while a Phi in the wrong colour is a scandal → the number of colours a consort must show grows with rank (law 5).
- Borrowing: dramas show consorts borrowing / being lent hairpins and ornaments for a feast (a favour that binds, sometimes a trap); a whole robe is never borrowed (fitted, embroidered with the owner's rank). The owner's 11b worry (screen must match the rule) agrees: only an ACCESSORY that is shown on her picture can be borrowed.
- "Đụng màu" (wearing the same colour as a superior at a gathering) is a classic drama slight; NOT built here (law 18: it goes to the parking lot if wanted).

## 3. Design taken from this pass (built in review/dresscode, numbers in BAL.dc)

2. Season fit by fabric (§1); cost Danh + Thái hậu goodwill per tuần worn off-season, larger by rank; NPCs the same.
3. Festival dress code = the table above (2 colours + taboo + strict); a consort shows the colours of her robe (her painting's main + secondary) and the MAIN colour of her trâm and of the hoa điền on her face (and of a borrowed accessory).
