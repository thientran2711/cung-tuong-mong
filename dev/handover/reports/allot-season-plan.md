## 1. History top-up (law 8) — only what the earlier reports lacked
Tags as before: [A] well attested · [B] commonly cited, numbers vary · [F] drama/novel · [G] game · [?] check before quoting. Written from knowledge, not re-verified against archives this session.
- **Cloth was a YEARLY / seasonal line, not monthly** [A/B]. In 《國朝宮史》 宮分 lists the cloth (蟒緞, 妝緞, 倭緞, 閃緞, 綾, 紡絲, 杭細, 綿紬, 高麗布, cotton wadding, furs for the top) is counted **per year** by rank; food, tea and fuel are counted per day/month. Clothes for the seasons were then **made by the court workshops** (造辦處 / 如意館-side tailors, 繡房 embroidery rooms), not sewn by the lady herself [B]. Servants got **四季衣裳** (clothes by the four seasons) [B] — the game already does this for servants (BAL.allot.srv.cloth months 1/4/7/10).
- **The change of dress (換季) was decreed by the court on fixed days**: the 3rd month (to summer hats/robes, 換戴涼帽) and the 9th month (to winter, 換戴暖帽), announced by the Board of Rites; the whole court changed together [A]. The game's silkAllot event already fires most in months 3 and 9 — the same two dates.
- **Wrong-season dress — punished?** For **officials**, appearing in the wrong dress at court could be reported as 失儀 (breach of ceremony) and bring a reprimand or fine [B]. For **palace women** I know of **no recorded penalty**; the sources show uniform change on the decreed day and social cost (looking poor, out of fashion, or "not cared for" by Nội vụ phủ) rather than punishment [?, no case found]. → **Plan: NO penalty.** Only a gain for dressing in the new season (law 3 gain-only is allowed).
- **Unused allowance was commuted to silver (折銀 / 折價)** [B]: allowances in kind could be paid as their silver value when not drawn — a standard Qing household-office practice (also for officials' grain). This is the history-backed answer to "it just piles up".
- **Tea goes stale** — tea of an old year was "陳茶" and lower-grade; damp tea turned musty [A, common practice]. The game already has the item Trà mốc (tramoc).
- **Charcoal**: counted by rank in 斤 per day in winter, more for higher ranks (紅羅炭 for the top, 黑炭 for the low) [B] — so for coal history supports **more count by rank**, not only finer grade (exception to law C, allowed by "unless history says otherwise").
- **Dramas** [F]: 《甄嬛傳》 — Nội vụ phủ sends **fabrics (料子) for the new season's clothes** and Hoa phi takes the finest Thục cẩm first; when Chân Hoàn falls out of favour her palace gets black smoky coal, and she sends her own red-gauze coal to the confined Thẩm Mi Trang (giving down surplus as a kindness). 《延禧攻略》 — garments are made in the **繡坊 (embroidery bureau)**, the consorts order, maids sew; old clothes are handed down. 《如懿傳》 — a cut allowance shows first as winter coal withheld. None shows a lady punished for wearing last season's dress; it is shown as shabbiness.
- **Games** [G]: *Stardew Valley* — surplus goes into the shipping bin for money (no item vanishes, it becomes coin); *The Sims* — food spoils, so stock cannot pile; *Animal Crossing* — limited storage, sell the rest. Lesson: **cap + convert to money + let perishables age**; never a silent delete.
