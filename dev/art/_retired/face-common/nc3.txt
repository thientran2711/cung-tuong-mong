A princess of the foreign kingdom of Nam Chiếu, young and noble, about 18, before entering the imperial palace, clearly a royal FOREIGN princess, splendid and elegant, never servant-like, never a maid. Her face: softly heart-shaped face with a gently rounded jaw and small dimples, wide-set bright eyes, straight soft brows, a small cupid's-bow mouth. Costume in the style of an ancient Japanese court princess (Heian): several thin flowing silk robes layered one over another, their edges showing at the collar, the sleeve openings and the hem in graded shades of wisteria lilac, pale lavender and white, the outer robe of soft wisteria-purple silk woven with small white wisteria and paulownia patterns, very wide long open sleeves, long pleated pale-crimson hakama-like trousers-skirt reaching the floor; her very long straight black hair falling loose down her back to the knees, tied loosely low with a thin white paper ribbon, a small gold hair ornament at the crown. Item: a folding fan of thin painted wooden slats with long coloured silk cords, held half-open in both hands at her chest.
