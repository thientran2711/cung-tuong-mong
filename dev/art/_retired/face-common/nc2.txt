A princess of the foreign kingdom of Nam Chiếu, young and noble, about 19, before entering the imperial palace, clearly a royal FOREIGN princess, splendid and elegant, never servant-like, never a maid. Her face: diamond-shaped face with delicate high cheekbones and a slim softly pointed chin, elongated gentle almond eyes, slim willow-leaf brows, a full soft mouth. Costume in the style of a Korean royal princess: a short jade-green silk jacket with long deep-rose ribbons tied at the chest and gold-stamped flower patterns, a very full high-waisted floor-length skirt of soft deep-rose silk billowing like a bell with gold-stamped borders, a long ornate tassel ornament (norigae) hanging from the ribbon, her hair parted in the middle and smoothed into a low braided bun at the nape, a long gold binyeo hairpin pushed HORIZONTALLY through the bun from side to side (lying flat, left to right, never vertical), a small jewelled ornament. Item: a round embroidered silk fan held in one hand at her waist.
