A princess of the foreign kingdom of Nam Chiếu, young and noble, about 19, before entering the imperial palace, clearly a royal FOREIGN princess, splendid and elegant, never servant-like, never a maid. Her face: soft round face with a small rounded chin, large long-lashed almond eyes with gently lifted outer corners, softly arched dark brows, a small full rosy mouth. Costume in the style of an ancient Indian (Thiên Trúc) royal princess, elegant and covered: a fitted short-sleeved silk bodice of soft saffron-gold under a long flowing pleated ankle-length lehenga skirt of soft coral-rose silk with fine gold-thread lotus and peacock-feather borders at the hem, a long sheer pale-gold silk veil draped from the back of her head over one shoulder and wrapped once around the waist (a dupatta), slim gold bangles, a gold waist chain; her long dark hair loose down her back in soft waves with a small jewelled gold ornament (maang tikka style) resting at the top of her parting on the HAIR, never on the forehead skin, gold jhumka earrings. Item: a small gold lotus-shaped oil lamp with a tiny flame held in both hands at her chest.
