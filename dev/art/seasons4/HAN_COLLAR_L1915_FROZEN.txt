EARLY (right after the daylight sentence):
NECKLINE (most important): her crossed collar opens WIDE and lies FLAT and SECURE on the shoulders, its edges reaching about TWO-THIRDS of the way out along both collarbones: the inner part of the collarbones visible, their outer ends covered, never slipping, never off the shoulders.
COLLAR CLAUSE (in the outfit):
the crossed collars of BOTH robes open WIDE: the collar edges lie flat and secure on the shoulders about two-thirds of the way out along the collarbones (inner part visible, outer ends covered) and meet a little above the bust in a broad V, the three bands lying flat along this wide opening over the shoulders (never narrow near the neck, never a tight high crossing, never off the shoulders)
BANDS (owner L1859/L1863/L1915; colours per rank):
THREE stacked WIDE gold-edged collar bands in the wide V (outer <MAIN> embroidered in gold, middle <SECONDARY>, innermost ivory white)
NO shoulder yoke / fringe. Rank ornament at the collar only, tiny and flat (L1915 "cực kỳ tiết chế").
