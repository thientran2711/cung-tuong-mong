A princess of the foreign kingdom of Bắc Địch, young and noble, about 20, before entering the imperial palace, clearly a royal FOREIGN princess, splendid and elegant, never servant-like, never a maid. Her face: oval face with gently high cheekbones, warm round-almond eyes, softly arched brows, a small rosy mouth. Costume in the style of an ancient Mongolian steppe princess: a long flowing ankle-length deel robe of light sky-blue silk fastened at the right shoulder with small coral buttons, a high soft collar, long sleeves ending in horseshoe-shaped cuffs, fine silver-thread cloud and knot embroidery along the lapel and hem, a wide pale-gold silk sash wound around the waist with long ends floating at the side, a tall elegant headdress of silver filigree set with coral and turquoise with strings of beads hanging beside her cheeks to her shoulders, her hair in two long sleek braids falling over her chest inside embroidered silk braid-sleeves. Item: a small bouquet of blue and white steppe wildflowers held in one hand at her chest.
