A Bắc Địch (northern steppe) princess, young and noble, about 20, before entering the imperial palace. Her face: oval face with gently high cheekbones, warm round-almond eyes, softly arched brows, a small rosy mouth. Hair: two long dark braids falling over her shoulders in front, a small jewelled headband of soft ivory fur set with coral and turquoise, turquoise-and-coral dangles at the temples. Outfit (the HOME costume of her steppe country, elegant, never poor): an ankle-length side-closing robe fastened on the left, of medium jade-green silk, a narrow soft ivory fur trim only at the collar and sleeve openings, fine embroidered flowers and small running horses along the hem, a coral-red silk sash with a silver buckle, soft leather boots. Item: a small bouquet of steppe wildflowers held in one hand at her chest.
