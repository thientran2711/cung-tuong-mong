A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 18. Her face: heart-shaped face with softly rounded cheeks, warm almond eyes with slightly upturned outer corners, soft willow-leaf brows, a small bow-shaped mouth. Hair: natural dark hair, half-up: the top twisted into a small knot tied with an apricot ribbon whose ends fall behind, two small gold flower hairpins, the rest loose to her waist. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of warm apricot with a pale apricot cream floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small orchids at the collar and cuffs, a soft silk sash with a small carnelian pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family warm apricot and gold, gold only as thin accents. Item: a sprig of plum blossom held near her chest in one hand.
