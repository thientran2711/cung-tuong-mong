A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 18. Her face: long soft oval face, long almond eyes, softly arched brows, a small rosy mouth with gently upturned corners. Hair: glossy pure BLACK (never brown), fully loose with a lavender-blue ribbon tied in a small bow at the back, a short string of small pearls pinned at the crown. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of lavender blue with a pale periwinkle floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small irises and leaves at the collar and cuffs, a soft silk sash with a small lapis pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family lavender blue and silver, silver only as thin accents. Item: a single iris stem held in one hand at her waist.
