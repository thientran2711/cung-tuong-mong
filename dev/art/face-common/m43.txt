A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 19. Her face: slender oval face, softly half-lidded demure eyes, fine thin brows, a small rosy mouth. Hair: dark brown, half-up: the top drawn back into a small low knot with a periwinkle ribbon and a small pearl flower hairpin, the rest loose. Outfit: a light silk beizi of periwinkle worn open over a cross-collar inner top and a pale silver-periwinkle floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small hydrangea florets along the front edges, a soft silk sash with a small silver pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family periwinkle and silver, silver only as thin accents. Item: a small embroidered pattern scroll rolled and held in one hand at her waist.
