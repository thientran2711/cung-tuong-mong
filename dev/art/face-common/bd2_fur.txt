A princess of the foreign kingdom of Bắc Địch, young and noble, about 19, before entering the imperial palace, clearly a royal FOREIGN princess, splendid and elegant, never servant-like, never a maid. Her face: heart-shaped face with a softly pointed chin, wide bright almond eyes, straight gently thick brows, full small lips. Costume in the style of an ancient Tibetan highland princess from a cold snowy land: a long wrap-around chuba robe of soft warm crimson-red silk brocade with small golden lotus patterns, its collar, front edge, sleeve cuffs and hem trimmed with a wide band of soft creamy-white fur, very long sleeves that drape softly, a broad apron of horizontal rainbow-striped silk in soft muted colours at the front, a slim silver belt with a round silver charm box on a cord at her chest; a small round hat of crimson silk with a soft white fur brim and a coral bead on top, her hair loose and long down her back in many fine thin braids threaded with coral and turquoise beads, large coral and turquoise earrings. Item: a small silver prayer wheel with a turquoise top held upright in one hand.
