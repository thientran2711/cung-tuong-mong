A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 16. Her face: round face with plump soft cheeks, smiling crescent eyes, short gently curved brows, a small wide smile. Hair: natural black hair, half-up: a small braided crown across the top tied at the back with jade-green ribbons and a little row of small white flower hairpins, the rest loose. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of pale jade green with a high-waisted white floor-length skirt tied just above the waist, fine delicate embroidered small cranes at the hem, a soft silk sash with a small white jade pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family pale jade green and white, silver only as thin accents. Item: a feathered shuttlecock held up in one hand at chest height.
