A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 17. Her face: round-oval face with a deep dimple in each cheek, bright large eyes, softly arched brows, a soft warm closed-lip smile. Hair: dark brown, half-up: two small knots at the back of the crown tied with golden-yellow ribbons, one small flower hairpin, the rest loose. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of pale golden yellow with a long ivory floor-length pleated skirt, fine delicate embroidered small camellias at the collar and hem, a soft silk sash with a small amber pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family pale golden yellow and ivory, gold only as thin accents. Item: a closed oil-paper parasol held upright beside her, one hand around the handle.
