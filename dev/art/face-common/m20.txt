A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 17. Her face: round face with rounded soft cheeks, smiling crescent eyes with slightly lifted corners, gently arched brows, a small upturned mouth. Hair: natural black hair, half-up: two small loops at the back of the crown tied with slate-blue ribbons and one small pearl hairpin, long loose hair behind. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of slate blue with a pale blue-grey floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small gardenias at the collar and cuffs, a soft silk sash with a small gold-rimmed jade pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family slate blue and gold, gold only as thin accents. Item: a small bamboo bird cage held up by its ring in one hand at her side.
