A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 16. Her face: round face with full apple cheeks, large round doe eyes, softly arched brows, a small rosy mouth with full lips. Hair: natural black hair, half-up: two thin plaits from the temples joined at the back of the head with a coral ribbon, one small pearl flower hairpin, the rest loose. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of coral red with a long pale coral pink floor-length pleated skirt, fine delicate embroidered small chrysanthemums at the collar and hem, a soft silk sash with a small red tassel knot, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family coral red and gold, gold only as thin accents. Item: an embroidered silk ball with short tassels held in both hands at her waist.
