A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 19. Her face: long slender oval face, gently downcast eyes, long fine brows, a small closed mouth. Hair: natural black hair, fully loose and straight with a thin sky-blue ribbon tied low at the nape and one small silver comb. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of sky blue with a pale silver-blue floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small cranes and clouds at the collar and cuffs, a soft silk sash with a small silver fish pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family sky blue and silver, silver only as thin accents. Item: a bamboo-slip book bundle held in both hands at her waist.
