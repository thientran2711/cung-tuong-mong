A Nam Chiếu (Nanzhao, Yunnan highland kingdom) princess, young and noble, about 19, before entering the imperial palace. Her face: diamond-shaped face with delicate high cheekbones and a slim softly pointed chin, elongated gentle almond eyes, slim willow-leaf brows, a full soft mouth. Hair: dark hair half-up, the top gathered into a small knot wound with one slim plait and set with small silver flowers, amber and coral beads and a fresh white camellia, the rest falling long and loose down her back with one thin plait in front of her shoulder, long silver chain earrings. Outfit (the HOME costume of her highland country, elegant, never poor, clearly NOT Chinese palace hanfu and NOT a maid's short top): an ankle-length soft coral-red silk dress with a small round collar and long fitted sleeves with embroidered cuffs, its full flowing skirt with one wide cream hem band embroidered with lotus and small geometric borders in coral, black and gold, over it a hip-length open cream silk vest edged with coral embroidery and a row of small silver buttons, a wide coral sash with a small silver plaque and short fringes, a slim silver collar ring with tiny silver bells; soft coral red with cream, silver and a little black as accents. Item: a blossoming branch of red camellias held gently in both hands at chest height.
