A princess of the foreign kingdom of Bắc Địch, young and noble, about 20, before entering the imperial palace, clearly a royal FOREIGN princess, splendid and elegant, never servant-like, never a maid. Her face: long oval face with a softly defined chin, large deep-set eyes with soft double lids, gracefully arched dark brows, a slim straight nose, a small rosy mouth. Costume in the style of an ancient Khitan (Liao) princess: a long round-collared robe of pale rose silk closing on the left, woven with small golden flower roundels and flying geese, slim sleeves under a sleeveless long open over-coat of ivory silk edged with a band of rose and gold embroidery, a soft red leather belt with small gold plaques and hanging ribbons, soft embroidered boots under the hem; her hair half-up: the top gathered under a small gold openwork crown with dangling pearls, the rest long and straight down her back, gold earrings with amber drops. Item: a white gyrfalcon feather fan with a gold handle held in one hand at her side.
