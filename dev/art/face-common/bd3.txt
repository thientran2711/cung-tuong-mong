A Bắc Địch (northern steppe) princess, young and noble, about 20, before entering the imperial palace. Her face: long oval face with a softly defined chin, large deep-set eyes with soft double lids, gracefully arched dark brows, a slim straight nose, a small rosy mouth. Hair: half-up, the top pinned into a small knot under a small round jewelled cap of rose silk with a silver rim, coral beads and a short white feather on one side, the rest falling straight and long down her back, long silver earrings with coral drops. Outfit (the HOME costume of her steppe country, elegant, never poor): an ankle-length side-closing robe fastened on the left, of soft rose-crimson silk woven with small deer and flower roundels, fine embroidered peonies and deer along the hem, the lapel and the sleeve cuffs, a narrow soft white fur edge only on the collar (a thin trim, not wintry), a wide embroidered pale-rose silk sash tied at the side with long ends, soft leather boots; soft rose crimson with pale rose, silver only as thin accents. Item: a small round frame drum painted with flowers, with coral tassels, held against her hip in one hand, the other hand lifted lightly to tap it.
