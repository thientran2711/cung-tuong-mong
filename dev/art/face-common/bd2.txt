A princess of the foreign kingdom of Bắc Địch, young and noble, about 19, before entering the imperial palace, clearly a royal FOREIGN princess, splendid and elegant, never servant-like, never a maid. Her face: heart-shaped face with a softly pointed chin, wide bright almond eyes, straight gently thick brows, full small lips. Costume in the style of an ancient Tibetan princess: a long wrap-around chuba robe of soft warm crimson-red silk brocade with small golden lotus patterns, very long sleeves that fall past her hands and drape softly, a broad apron of horizontal rainbow-striped silk in soft muted colours at the front, a fine gold-embroidered border at the collar and hem, a slim silver belt with a round silver charm box (gau) on a cord at her chest; her hair loose and long down her back in many fine thin braids, a headpiece of coral and turquoise beads across the crown, large coral and turquoise earrings. Item: a small silver prayer wheel with a turquoise top held upright in one hand.
