A Bắc Địch (northern steppe) princess, young and noble, about 19, before entering the imperial palace. Her face: heart-shaped face with a softly pointed chin, wide bright almond eyes, straight gently thick brows, full small lips. Hair: many small fine braids gathered into one long low ponytail down her back, bound with a soft red silk cord, a slim gold chain headband with small gold coins and turquoise beads resting on top of her hair behind the hairline (never on the forehead), gold drop earrings. Outfit (the HOME costume of her steppe country, elegant, never poor): an ankle-length side-closing robe fastened on the left, of warm honey-amber silk woven with small cloud patterns, narrow sleeves widening a little at the cuffs, fine embroidered wild flowers and grass along the hem and the lapel edge, a light sleeveless open over-vest of deep cream silk with a thin embroidered border, a slim leather-and-silk belt with small gold plaques and hanging tassels, soft leather boots; no fur, no hat; warm honey amber with deep cream, gold only as thin accents. Item: a short riding whip with a carved silver handle and a long red silk tassel held loosely in one hand down by her side, the other hand resting on her belt.
