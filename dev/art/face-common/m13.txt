A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 18. Her face: soft oval face, almond eyes with slightly upturned corners, thin gently arched brows, a small rosy mouth with a soft cupid's bow. Hair: black, fully loose and long, a thin lilac ribbon tied at the back of the head and one small pearl flower comb above the left ear. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of lilac with a high-waisted pale silvery lilac floor-length skirt tied just above the waist, fine delicate embroidered small osmanthus flowers at the hem, a soft silk sash with a small amethyst pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family lilac and silver, silver only as thin accents. Item: a small wisteria spray held in one hand near her chest.
