A Nam Chiếu (Nanzhao, Yunnan highland kingdom) princess, young and noble, about 20, before entering the imperial palace. Her face: round soft face with gently full cheeks, large clear doe eyes, gentle crescent brows, a small rosy mouth. Hair: dark hair in a rounded bun at the crown with two small plaits wound around it, studded with small pearls, silver flowers and turquoise beads, slim silver tassel earrings. Outfit (the HOME costume of her highland country, elegant, never poor, clearly NOT Chinese palace hanfu and NOT a maid's short top): an ankle-length indigo-blue silk dress with a small stand collar fastened at the right side by small silver buttons, its long skirt flowing with an embroidered hem band of camellias, over it a knee-length sleeveless ivory over-dress richly embroidered with camellias and edged in indigo, a silver chain belt with a small silver plaque, a slim silver neck ring; soft indigo blue like #6A86B4 with ivory, silver accents. Item: a folded fan of ivory silk painted with red camellias held half-open in one hand near her collarbone.
