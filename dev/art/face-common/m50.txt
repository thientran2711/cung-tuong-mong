A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 16. Her face: round face with wide soft cheeks, bright crescent eyes, gently curved brows, a big sweet smile. Hair: black, half-up: a small knot at the crown with soft pink ribbons and a thin pearl hair chain, the rest loose with short side locks. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of soft pink with a pearl white floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small hydrangea blossoms at the collar and cuffs, a soft silk sash with a small pearl pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family soft pink and pearl white, silver only as thin accents. Item: a round gauze fan painted with a lotus, held in one hand at her waist.
