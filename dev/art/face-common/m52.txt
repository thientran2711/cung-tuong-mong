A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 16. Her face: small round face with a little pointed chin, large bright eyes, gently arched brows, a sweet small mouth. Hair: black, half-up: a soft pink-lilac ribbon tied in a big bow at the back of the crown, two small flower hairpins, the rest loose. Outfit: a short light cross-collar silk jacket of pink lilac over a pale lilac floor-length pleated skirt, fine delicate embroidered small wisteria and butterflies at the collar and hem, a soft silk sash with a small gold-rimmed amethyst pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family pink lilac and gold, gold only as thin accents. Item: a hydrangea bloom held in both hands near her chest.
