A Nam Chiếu (Nanzhao, Yunnan highland kingdom) princess, young and noble, about 18, before entering the imperial palace. Her face: softly heart-shaped face with a gently rounded jaw and small dimples, wide-set bright eyes, straight soft brows, a small cupid's-bow mouth. Hair: long dark hair worn loose down her back, two thin plaits from the temples joined at the back of her head with a slim band of white silk woven with silver thread, small silver butterfly hairpins with dangles, silver hoop earrings. Outfit (the HOME costume of her highland country, elegant, never poor, clearly NOT Chinese palace hanfu and NOT a maid's short top): a fitted long-sleeved lilac silk blouse with a small round collar and a row of small silver buttons down one side, over a long flowing ankle-length wrap skirt of pale silver-grey silk woven with lilac and silver bands, a belt of linked small silver plaques at the waist, a fine lilac embroidered border of small orchids at the cuffs and the skirt hem; soft lilac violet with pale silver-grey, silver accents. Item: a long string of amber and turquoise beads hanging from one raised hand at shoulder height, the other hand lightly touching the beads.
