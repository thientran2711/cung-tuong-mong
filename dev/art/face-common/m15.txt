A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 19. Her face: slender oval face with a small narrow chin, long elegant phoenix-like eyes, fine high brows, a small rosy mouth. Hair: natural dark hair, half-up: the top drawn into a small low knot with a blush ribbon and a short pearl hairpin, the rest long and loose. Outfit: a light silk beizi of blush pink worn open over a cross-collar inner top and a pearl white floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small pear blossoms along the front edges, a soft silk sash with a small pearl pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family blush pink and pearl white, silver only as thin accents. Item: a jade hairpin box of pale lacquer held in both hands in front of her.
