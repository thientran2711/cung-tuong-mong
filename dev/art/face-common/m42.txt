A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 18. Her face: oval face with flushed soft cheeks, lively upturned eyes, gently arched brows, a warm wide smile. Hair: natural black hair, half-up: two small braided loops at the crown tied with peach-coral ribbons and one small flower hairpin, the rest loose. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of peach coral with a high-waisted pale peach floor-length skirt tied just above the waist, fine delicate embroidered small peach blossoms and leaves at the hem, a soft silk sash with a small coral bead pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family peach coral and gold, gold only as thin accents. Item: a sprig of apricot blossom held near her chest in one hand.
