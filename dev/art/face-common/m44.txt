A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 17. Her face: round-oval face, soft wide eyes, gently arched brows, a small prim mouth. Hair: black, one braid down her back from the crown, tied with blush-pink ribbons, the side locks loose in front of the ears, one small pink flower hairpin. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of ivory with a blush pink floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small begonia sprays at the collar and cuffs, a blush silk sash with a small rose quartz pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family ivory and blush pink, gold only as thin accents. Item: a small porcelain vase holding a single flower sprig, held in both hands in front of her.
