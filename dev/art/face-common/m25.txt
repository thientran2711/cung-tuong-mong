Hair: natural black hair, half-up: two thin plaits from the temples crossed at the back and tied with violet ribbons, one small silver flower hairpin, the rest loose. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of lavender violet with a pale lilac floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small violets at the collar and cuffs, a soft silk sash with a small amethyst bead pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family lavender violet and silver, silver only as thin accents. Item: a round silk fan painted with butterflies, with a short tassel, held near her chest.
