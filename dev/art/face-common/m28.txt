A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 16. Her face: small heart-shaped face with a little button chin, large round eyes, gently arched brows, a small rosy bow mouth. Hair: natural black hair, half-up: two small loops at the crown tied with coral ribbon bows, one small pearl flower hairpin, the rest loose. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of coral pink with a long ivory floor-length pleated skirt, fine delicate embroidered small rabbits and grasses at the hem, a soft silk sash with a small coral pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family coral pink and ivory, gold only as thin accents. Item: a small paper pinwheel held up in one hand near her shoulder.
