Hair: black, half-up: a small braided band across the crown tied at the back with a mint ribbon bow, two small pearl hairpins, the rest loose. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of pale mint green with a long ivory floor-length pleated skirt, fine delicate embroidered small dragonflies at the collar and hem, a soft silk sash with a small jade cicada pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family pale mint green and ivory, silver only as thin accents. Item: a small swallow-shaped paper kite held by its frame in both hands in front of her.
