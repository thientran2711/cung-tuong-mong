A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 16. Her face: round face with soft baby cheeks, big round eyes, short straight brows, a small upturned smile. Hair: natural black hair, half-up: two small knots at the sides of the crown tied with peach-pink ribbons, the rest loose and long. Outfit: a light silk beizi of peach pink worn open over a cross-collar inner top and a pale peach floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small pomegranate flowers along the front edges, a soft silk sash with a small red bead tassel, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family peach pink and gold, gold only as thin accents. Item: a small pellet drum held up by its handle in one hand.
