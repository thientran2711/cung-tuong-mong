A young unmarried noble girl from a scholar's family (thiếu nữ khuê các), about 18. Her face: heart-shaped face tapering to a small pointed chin, almond eyes with clearly upturned outer corners, high softly arched brows, a slightly fuller lower lip. Hair: natural dark hair, half-up: the top in a small soft knot wrapped with a rose-pink ribbon and one small gold butterfly hairpin, the rest loose with soft side locks. Outfit: a light cross-collar silk ruqun of rose pink with a pale blush rose floor-length skirt, fine delicate embroidered small auspicious clouds at the cuffs, a soft silk sash with a small rose quartz pendant, long soft sleeves; her signature colour family rose pink and gold, gold only as thin accents. Item: a pipa held upright against her body with both hands.
