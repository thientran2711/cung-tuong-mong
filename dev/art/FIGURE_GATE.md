### THE RULE
1. For anything listed below as APPROVED: run the listed builder command and paste its output UNCHANGED, with the listed references. Never write a new form, never add or remove a sentence, never "improve" it.
2. The ONLY things that may change: (a) the HELD ITEM (from the status/rank list: FIGURE_GATE 3f for consort ranks, FIGURE_GATE H for other statuses); (b) the POSE (FIGURE_GATE D.3a: spec5 POSES copied, frontal or three-quarter only, never back-turned/looking back).
3. CALM WITH RANK AND AGE: the higher the rank and the older the age, the calmer and more composed the pose and the hand–item interaction: low ranks / young may be lively (a step, a turn of the wrist, sleeves moving) (canon overwrite 18/11 ~23:25Z real; OWNER (verbatim): "bậc thấp của phi tần thì ok. Cung nữ hay người hầu thì tay áo rộng khoảng 2 gang tay, dáng được sinh động"): low consort ranks may have flowing sleeves and a lively pose; MAIDS AND SERVANTS (cung nữ / người hầu) wear sleeves about 2 hand-spans wide ("khoảng 2 gang tay") and may have a lively pose; high ranks (t6–t8, Thái hậu, Nữ đế) and a51/a65 = serene, dignified, small restrained gestures (a hand may extend, but calmly); NEVER wide-spread arms or legs, dancing, twirling, skipping or any boisterous movement for them. The item is held/used the way a lady of that rank and age would.
4. Not listed here = NOT approved = sample only, never mass. A new owner approval is added here the same hour (Foreman C), with the builder command, the build.py commit and the approved sheet.
5. Every brief to a painter points to this file; reviewers FAIL any gen whose prompt differs from the listed form in anything but item/pose.
