A brand-new painting of a natural, lived-in, richly furnished room in an ancient Chinese imperial palace, painted exactly like image 1 (the bedroom): same hand, same brushwork, same soft painterly finish, same natural light, same depth with furniture, curtains and objects arranged through the room; the soft painted style of our game, never glossy, never over-detailed, never a shiny 3D render. COLOUR LEVEL (owner L1669: the 'rất nhẹ' look, the same colours only a little fresher): clear, clean colours like the girl in image 2, the same colours as the room in image 1, only a little fresher and richer: every colour clearly readable, no grey veil, no dull haze and no muted wash over the picture; never faded, never washed out, never grey or dull, never over-saturated, never neon or glaring. Wide panoramic view, summer morning. ROOM: Hàm An cung chính điện reception room, the sitting room where a Tần (an imperial consort of the fourth rank, below a Phi) who heads this palace and lives here alone in ancient China receives her guests, a real room in daily use, no bed anywhere, seen with THREE walls: the back wall straight across the middle of the picture and the left and right side walls running towards the viewer in clear perspective, meeting it at square corners, like the wall layout of image 3: pillars, lattice windows in the LEFT side wall onto a sunny garden; against the middle of the back wall, square to it and seen straight on, the host seat, the large clear main subject: a luohan daybed of carved light walnut-brown elm wood with a three-sided panel back, soft pink silk cushions and a small low kang table with a tea set in its middle, before a folding screen painted with orchids and butterflies; in the RIGHT side wall the four-lobed begonia-shaped doorway in a key-fret lattice partition onto an inner room; along the left window wall, square to it, a pair of armchairs with gently curved yoke-shaped top rails and soft pink cushions and a small tea table between them; blue-and-white pots of orchids on stands, a small incense burner on a side table, a low shelf with books and scrolls, hanging paper lanterns with tassels. Hàm An cung signature style, the same in every room of this palace: orchids (hoa lan) are its flower, painted on the screens, embroidered on the silks and in the pots; motifs of butterflies among orchids carved in the beams and furniture; light warm walnut-brown elm wood with small blue-and-white porcelain plaques; armchairs with gently curved yoke-shaped top rails; a four-lobed begonia-shaped doorway in a key-fret lattice partition.  RANK COLOUR (Tần, owner B24): soft pink like #E3A3B8, a soft medium pink, is the one rank colour of the room, woven through the seat cushions, the window curtains and the lantern tassels, with ivory and soft jade-green as its quiet partners; no purple, no orange, no red silks. COLOUR TONE (owner: the room has the same colour tone as the outfit of the woman who lives here): the room's colours have the SAME colour strength as her outfit and as the colour strip in image 4: rich, clear and fully coloured, like a room dressed in the very colours of her clothes; never paler, never greyer, never faded, milky or washed out, and never neon or glaring either; the pink of image 4 at its full strength is the main colour of the silks, curtains and cushions, with its ivory and soft jade-green partners, and the wood keeps its own rich polished colour; bright neutral white daylight, no warm golden or amber cast. TẦN level, the simplest of the three chính điện versions yet one step richer than a lower consort's room: polished wood with fine carving and some jade-green inlay, embroidered silk, a few jade and porcelain ornaments, a little gilt edging; pretty and noble, never poor, never grand. No phoenix anywhere (no phoenix carving, embroidery, figure or pendant), no imperial yellow, no golden-yellow fabrics. No Western sofa, no upholstered couch. ROOM SHAPE (owner Lượt 491): the room reads rectilinear with square corners, straight walls and straight beams, never a curved hall, never furniture in an arc or a curve; every piece of furniture stands in clear straight lines along the walls, square to the walls, orderly, never scattered and never at a slant. Image 3 is a guide ONLY for the wall layout, the camera distance, the floor amount and the people scale, NOT for its colours, furniture or arrangement: this room has its own arrangement as written here. FLOOR AMOUNT (owner Lượt 668, measured): the same open floor as image 3: the front of the platform step and the fronts of the chair rows stand at about five sixths of the picture height, so the open floor in front is a narrow strip of about one sixth of the picture height, never more. CAMERA CLOSE (owner 'sàn còn rộng quá'): the camera stands only a few steps in front of the furniture, so the room and its furniture fill the picture down to that line; never a wide empty floor in the middle of the picture. NAME-TAG STRIP: the bottom eighth of the picture, across the whole width and in the bottom-left corner too, is bare floor only: no basket, box, stool, pot, incense burner, slippers or any other object stands there. CAMERA AND SCALE: eye level of a standing woman, the same camera distance, eye level and natural people scale as image 3 (another approved room, used only as the guide written above); no ceiling in the picture: the walls, pillars and hanging lanterns run out of the top edge. The room is made for four standing women placed later in front of the furniture: each woman about three quarters of the picture height tall, her feet at about seven eighths of the picture height, spread across the width (centres at about 15, 36, 56 and 76 % of the width). So the furniture stands BEHIND where they will stand: the bases of the beds, seats, tables and stands in that band sit no lower than seven eighths of the picture height; below that, the floor of the room simply continues to the bottom edge, open, nothing standing on it. The corners are furnished, never bare, and the left part of the room near the viewer holds only small, low objects, never a large piece close up at the left edge. The floor strip under the feet (the bottom tenth of the picture) is calm and evenly lit plain floor, without busy pattern or strong reflections, so three short lines of small name text placed there later read clearly. FLOOR: warm polished wooden floorboards, no large rug or carpet covering it. LIGHT: morning daylight from the right, like the light on the girl in image 2: bright neutral white daylight, clean white light, no warm golden or amber cast, lanterns unlit. Natural and furnished, never an empty stage; people will be placed in it later. Empty of people, no text, no writing, no characters.
