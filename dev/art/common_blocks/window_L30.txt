WINDOWS (palace rule L30, open and bright): no fabric curtains, drapes, swags, valances or tie-backs at any window, yet the windows are OPEN and full of daylight, never a solid closed wall of paper: carved wooden lattice frames (bubu-jin, ice-crack, key-fret) filled with translucent summer silk gauze through which the trees and garden outside are clearly seen, one or two window panels propped or swung open on their hinges showing the green garden, and small clear glass panes set in the lattice where they fit; bright daylight pours in through the open panels and the gauze. Doors are lattice doors; only a padded brocade door curtain (winter) or a fine painted bamboo blind (summer) may hang in a door frame; roll-up bamboo or reed blinds only under the eaves. Silk or gauze bed curtains hooked back with metal hooks are allowed and welcome; soft silk hangings tied back are allowed only inside carved openwork partition arches, never on the exterior window wall. The room stays rich: densely carved lacquered or gilded lattice, embroidered bed curtains, silk cushions, screens, curio shelves, porcelain, lanterns. Never european curtains, window drapes, curtain rods, pelmets, ruffles or net curtains.
