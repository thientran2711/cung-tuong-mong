WINDOWS (palace rule L30, structure-true): keep the room's architecture exactly as it is: every wall, column, pillar, niche, shrine, door, window opening and every object hung on a wall (maps, scrolls, paintings) stays where it is, and hung objects stay on solid wall. No fabric curtains, drapes, swags, valances or tie-backs at REAL windows: where a drape hung at a real window, show that same window's carved wooden lattice (bubu-jin, ice-crack, key-fret) with translucent silk gauze or window paper, lightly glowing with daylight, the garden softly seen through it. Never turn a wall, column, niche or shrine into a window, never add a new window or opening, and never swing a window panel open unless that window was already open. A hanging on a wall, a niche, a shrine or a column is not a window curtain: it stays, or becomes a sensible silk wall hanging. Doors are lattice doors; only a padded brocade door curtain (winter) or a fine painted bamboo blind (summer) may hang in a door frame; roll-up bamboo or reed blinds only under the eaves. Silk or gauze bed curtains hooked back with metal hooks are allowed and welcome; soft silk hangings tied back are allowed inside carved openwork partition arches. The room stays rich: densely carved lacquered or gilded lattice, embroidered bed curtains, silk cushions, screens, curio shelves, porcelain, lanterns. Never european curtains, window drapes, curtain rods, pelmets, ruffles or net curtains.
