WINDOWS (palace rule L30): every window is carved wooden lattice (bubu-jin, ice-crack, key-fret) backed by glowing white paper or pale gauze, optionally small glass panes in the lattice centre; NO fabric curtains, drapes, swags, valances or tie-backs at any window. Doors are lattice doors; only a padded brocade door curtain (winter) or a fine painted bamboo blind (summer) may hang in a door frame; roll-up bamboo or reed blinds only under the eaves. Silk or gauze bed curtains hooked back with metal hooks are allowed and welcome; soft silk hangings tied back are allowed only inside carved openwork partition arches, never on the exterior window wall. The room stays rich: densely carved lacquered or gilded lattice, warm diffused light through the paper, embroidered bed curtains, silk cushions, screens, curio shelves, porcelain, lanterns. Never european curtains, window drapes, curtain rods, pelmets, ruffles or net curtains.
