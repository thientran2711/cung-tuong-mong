## 2. History and drama precedents (court-wide events, how often)

"Court-wide" = ordered by the throne and obeyed by the whole palace. Frequencies are for one reign; ~ = approximate.

- **Sứ thần / triều cống.** Minh: Ryukyu, Joseon, Annam kept regular tribute schedules (Joseon 3-4 times a year in Ming-Qing, Annam every ~3 years, Ryukyu ~every 2 years). Qing: Joseon yearly; Western embassies rarer (Macartney 1793, Amherst 1816; Jesuits at Kangxi's court, clocks and mirrors as gifts, the very gifts in `j67 s_su`). Đại Việt/Nguyễn sent and received missions to Qing every 2-4 years and received Chân Lạp / Xiêm envoys. In Chân Hoàn truyện style dramas the envoy banquet appears 1-2 times a series. Verdict: common (every year or two), always a banquet + Lễ bộ + Thái hậu protocol.
- **Tuyển tú.** Qing Bannermaiden draft (Xiunü) every 3 years, spring, for Eight Banners daughters, which is exactly the `KC.ty=3` the game already uses. Minh: large drafts at accession and on the Emperor's wedding, not strictly regular. Nguyễn: draft on need. In Chân Hoàn truyện the first draft opens the story; Diên Hi công lược and Như Ý truyện reuse the "new batch arrives, old favourites shaken" beat. Effect on everyone: new rivals and rooms, Hoàng hậu / Thái hậu set the list, maids reassigned, eunuchs register them.
- **Thọ yến / Vạn thọ.** Qing: Kangxi's 60th (1713) and Qianlong's 60th/70th/80th with embassies and elder banquets (千叟宴 "yến ngàn lão", Kangxi 1713 and 1722, Qianlong 1785 and 1796); Thái hậu's birthday (Empress Dowager Chongqing 60th, 70th, 80th under Qianlong) was a full-court event. Yearly Vạn thọ tiết for the Emperor's birthday (every year, minor) with big milestone years (50/60) rare. Nguyễn: Thọ tiết yearly; Tứ tuần / lục tuần at ages 40/60 rare. Verdict: yearly baseline exists; milestone version once or twice per reign.
- **Hỏa hoạn.** Forbidden City palaces burned repeatedly (Ming: Three Great Halls 1421, rebuilt 1440; 1557 fire; 1597/1625 fires; Qing: Taihe-area and Cining palace fires, Yuanmingyuan 1860 is war). Palace fire in the dry season was a standard drama beat (Chân Hoàn truyện fire at Yu-ying; Diên Hi: Ngự thiện / cold palace blaze). Roughly once per generation for a whole hall; kitchen/small-block fires yearly. Effect: Emperor moves residence, maid details, "xin tội" edict, Nội vụ phủ audits, arson suspicion falls on a rival.
- **Thiên tai (lũ, hạn, nạn đói).** Qing court received flood/drought memorials almost every year; "major" reliefs (Emperor reduces his own table, sends silver, waives taxes) ~ every 3-6 years. Minh Wanli-era northern drought 1630s. Đại Việt: Lý-Trần "cầu mưa" rites, Nguyễn "phát chẩn", "cầu đảo". Effect: Emperor mood, Hộ bộ and Kho lúa, "giảm thiện" (reduce table), tiệc bị hủy, palace pays tribute (j71 `v` text asks the harem to give). Rite of rain prayer by Emperor at the altar.
- **Dịch bệnh.** Smallpox: Shunzhi died of it (1661), Kangxi survived it (so the court preferred him). Qing quarantine of palace against "đậu" (variolation introduced 1680s). Nguyễn: dịch tả (cholera) 1820 at the start of Minh Mạng reign, thousands died, court rites. Palace-wide lockdown, Thái y apportionment, rare ~ once or twice per reign.
- **Nhật thực / sao chổi / điềm.** Chinese tradition: eclipse = Emperor's failing; the Emperor issues "tội kỷ chiếu" (self-blame edict), cuts table, officials "cứu hộ" (rescue ritual, drums). Frequent in Han-Tang, still ordered in Ming-Qing; Khâm Thiên giám (Kangxi era with Jesuits Verbiest predicting, 1660s Calendar Case) is exactly the office the game names in `j7:99`. Eclipses visible and recorded ~1-2 a year; court-significant ones less often.
- **Quốc tang.** Death of Emperor / Thái hậu / Hoàng hậu / senior prince. Qing: 100 days no weddings, no music, no banquets, officials in white or blue, no shaving (the 1748 case after Empress Xiaoxianchun (Qianlong's first wife) where officials who shaved during the mourning were punished; I recall it clearly enough to cite as a precedent, mark ~). Nguyễn: 3 years nominal, 27 months in practice, then shortened. Drama: Chân Hoàn truyện, Diên Hi (Fucha empress's death sets the plot), Như Ý (Hiếu Hiền Hoàng hậu's death). This is the strongest fit: the game's j92 already punishes red/yellow dress in quốc tang.
- **Đại xá / ân điển.** On accession, new era name, birth of an heir, lập hậu, victory, or after disaster: pardons, tax waivers, rank rises for consorts (Qing "thưởng ban" on a prince's birth: consorts' ranks rise). Frequent in a reign: ~ 4-10 times. Effect: cold-palace consorts may be forgiven, punished consorts released.
- **Tế lễ, thu săn, tuần du, xuất chinh.** Qing Mulan autumn hunt (~ annual from 1681, Kangxi and Qianlong ~ every year with a selected group of consorts and princes; the harem and Thái hậu remain). Southern tours: Kangxi 6 (1684-1707), Qianlong 6 (1751-1784), ~ one per 5-6 years, with consorts selected to accompany, 2-4 months away. Campaigns: Kangxi's 3 Galdan campaigns (1690, 1696, 1697), Qianlong's wars; Yongle's northern campaigns 1410-1424 (5 in 14 years); Đại Việt kings (Lê Thánh Tông 1470 Champa) personally led. Tế Giao (Nam Giao in Nguyễn every 3 years, Qing Altar of Heaven yearly at winter solstice), tế Xã Tắc, tế Tiên nông (Emperor ploughs, spring, Qing yearly), Tảo mộ at Thanh minh. In dramas: "Hoàng thượng đi xa" is the canonical window when the palace's schemes bloom (Chân Hoàn truyện: Hoàng thượng at the hunt while Hoa phi plots). Effect: no thị tẩm, Thái hậu / Hoàng hậu run the harem, selected consorts accompany, NPC plots move.
- **Similar games.** Reigns of Heaven / Qing-court sims and "Chinese palace" gacha-visual-novels use: random-event decks with tributary ranks and calendar events; "Thiên Thu Huyền Mạch"-type sims run a calendar (tuyển tú every 3 years, thọ yến, tế tự) rather than dice. Crusader-Kings-style "world/realm events" fire for all characters and give each character an AI-driven reaction, which is the model OD:L1587 (agents' own deeds, shared world) asks for. Takeaway: calendar-bound big events (tuyển tú, tế, thọ) + a rare disaster deck that SCALES with real state (Kho lúa, season, weather, Hoàng thượng age) beats pure dice.

## 3. Proposed world events (11)

Common spec (one layer, reuses everything below it):
- One WORLD WINDOW record in the shared world state (same level as `S.festDone`, a co-op world key): `{k, t0, t1, scope, cause}`. Starts from the monthly roll or a calendar rule, runs N tuần, ends by itself.

- Two-way law: NPC agents may CAUSE the event (arsonist, hoarder, a rival who sabotages the envoy gift) and always REACT (plan weights per window) and the player may change the window outcome by what she does.
