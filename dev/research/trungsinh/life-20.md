### Pacing
35 months in ~8 min — fastest life; capped stats, only 5–6 monthly templates. The pregnancy fell in the 3rd game year and ended the life 9 months later, so the whole run was effectively a countdown.

### 3 lines
- The game's most brutal rule: an early pregnancy with no odds and no way to prepare ends the life even at full health — the owner's "khó sinh" question is answered: yes, it can kill, and it showed no percentage.
- Cáo bệnh xin khất on the first summons is the only protection; the autopilot (and a naive player) accepts.
- A fully capped Ngự nữ (Dung/Trí/Tài/Hoạt 100) died at 18y11m with no storyline — a fast, cheap, ruthless ending.
