### Pacing
101 months ≈ 38 real min. 7 calm years (HP ~80), then a 14-month collapse. Events every month; 1 in 3 is a forced loss card.

### 3 lines
1. Even a careful maid who never climbs dies young (24): the game guarantees a late harm chain that drains HP faster than any free healing.
2. The bully who rises in rank (Vệ Nhã Tâm, Thải nữ → Bảo lâm) is the closest thing to a rival story — and it is produced by one template + the NPC's own promotions.
3. The cause sentence quotes the last harm card, so the end card reads like the final line of a story.
