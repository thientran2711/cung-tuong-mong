### Pacing
~1.5 s per action, ~10 s/month on autopilot. Events nearly every month (1–2 modals); long cutscenes only at yearly feasts. Quiet stretches: none — filler templates repeat (water, tea, silk-ruined, rumours) with new names. Fast and shallow until 18y; then four major beats in 14 months.

### 3 lines
1. Fastest road from maid to Bảo lâm: one painting audition + emperor's night summons.
2. Death came from a quiet attrition meter (Sức khỏe) the game never warned about; heal cost vs. random side effects makes a poor-maid spiral.
3. Villains are one template with rotating NPC names; only the cat-attack scarring felt like a real story beat.
