### Pacing
~12 months per 8 real minutes; events every month; feast every January (7 modals). A pregnancy covers ~3 real minutes.

### 3 lines
1. Pregnancy came from a night when the Emperor disliked her — conception is not tied to love, and it immediately raised envy by 14.
2. Envy drives a chain: trial → demotion → harm to the pregnancy → death 10 months after conception. It reads as a story even though every beat is a generic card.
3. The epithet «Trăng lạnh cô cung» turns a miserable life into something you'd screenshot.
