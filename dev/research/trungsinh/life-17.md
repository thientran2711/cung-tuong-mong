### Tooling mistake worth knowing (affects the death)
After a manual visit to the Hoàng cung tab (18y1m) the page stayed on that tab; my autopilot reads Sức khỏe from the home tab, so it read 100 and **stopped inserting Y quan** for the remaining ~2 years, while its log lines were garbage. This very likely caused the health death. The game itself does not auto-return to the home tab after a month. The map visit therefore cost the life, not the game's difficulty alone.

### Pacing
50 months in ~17 min incl. thinking. An event modal nearly every month; ~1 in 3 months a real choice card, rest forced «Một bước chông chênh»/health hits. Health decay is invisible until the end card.

### 3 lines
- A maid who studies (Trí 79, Tài 92 by 18y) gets promoted by exam only; no romance or court story reaches a maid without a painting/emperor hook, so the middle of the life is chore events.
- The ending is the same «Sức khỏe cạn kiệt» line as life 16: death lines do not name the cause (poison, overwork) even though the log shows it.
- Lesson for the next lives: keep the UI on the home tab, Y quan whenever Sức khỏe < 65, and take the Hoàng cung visits only between months.
