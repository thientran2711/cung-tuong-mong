### 3 lines
- The maid path is a calm 9-year chore life ending in a polite «Xuất cung»: no villain, no romance, no death, and a card that finally shows «Hưởng thọ» and «Con cái».
- The only decision with meaning is the age-25 choice (leave / stay as Nữ quan); everything before it is exam grinding.
- Silver is the hidden throttle: Y quan costs 15 bạc, so a maid without silver cannot heal.
