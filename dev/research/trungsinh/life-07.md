### Pacing
~9 s real per month with 5 actions; an event card roughly every 1–2 months; quiet stretches none, but after year 12 events are the SAME ~40 cards on loop — the life felt long and repetitive; no new story after ~year 15 except exams, reign change, mentor illness.
### 3 lines
1. Maid origin = no romance/pregnancy/child content at all; story is a bully-and-counter-frame loop.
2. The «schemer» ending tag «Bóng người sau rèm» was granted automatically from ≥8 scheme successes style play, with «Người giữ lòng lành» at the same time — tags stack, not exclusive.
3. Death at 54 was quiet and unwarned: the end-of-life is a timer, not a story beat.
