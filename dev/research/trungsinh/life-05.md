### Pacing
19 months in 7 real minutes. Fastest life of the 20 so far. Restart from this end card to the next identity card = 1 tap (+ optional rolls).

### 3 lines
1. A careless player dies in 7 real minutes at 17: two failed bridges/lanterns and one failed self-care are enough.
2. Death costs nothing permanent and still pays +1 point, so dying fast is not punished — it is part of the loop.
3. The quit button exists but is fenced: no point, and the confirm card spells out what is lost.
