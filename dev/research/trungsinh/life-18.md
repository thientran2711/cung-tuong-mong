### Pacing
73 months in ~52 min real, ~30 min of it playing. Months 1–36 were mostly stat-building (each 10–15 s); the emperor/prince storyline started at 17y and produced a rare-event every 1–3 months; the pregnancy/birth fell inside one year.

### 3 lines
- The game hides a real plot (emperor-prince pact, prince as father) behind a roll of the dice: nothing before 19y2 foreshadows it.
- This life is the first with a pregnancy: no khó sinh danger appeared because Đố kỵ stayed low; the warning text names Đố kỵ as the risk.
- The 50 % elopement choice is an honest odds card with three named outcomes (together / lãnh cung / death); a player who declines stays safe.
