### Pacing
First 2 years eventful (frame-up, cold palace, summons, exam). After year 3 the same ~45 cards loop; the life was mostly waiting for the emperor to die.
### 3 lines
1. Biggest story beat of all my lives = the framed-by-the-moonlight choice (kneel / refuse) — a real branch with a named cost.
2. A single early random penalty (scratched face) silently blocked favour for 17 years and I never got a warning card — frustrating, but a great «cause and consequence» seed.
3. The emperor's natural death ended the story with a one-line epilogue; no queen/child arc was reachable from a stuck Bảo lâm.
