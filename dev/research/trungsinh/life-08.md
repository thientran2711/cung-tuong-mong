### Take-away for our game
- The game closes a life instantly with a ONE-PARAGRAPH TIME-SKIP EPILOGUE (marriage at 20, 3 children, prosperity, death at 55) — a cheap, already-written «closure» template the owner liked; the epilogue is a generated summary of the rest of her life, not played.
- Failing the entry test is not death: it is a choice (maid / go home), both closing or changing the life.
- Achievement popup «Con nhà danh giá» fires at birth when the origin is noble (+1 điểm luân hồi).
