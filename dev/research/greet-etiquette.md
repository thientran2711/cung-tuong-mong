# Court greeting etiquette (Ming/Qing + palace dramas) — expert check for GREET-MEET (owner OD:L1327 rule)

## Findings
1. **Self-naming goes upward.** A consort below Quý phi calls herself *thần thiếp* (臣妾) and, meeting a superior for the first time, states her title and name ("Thần thiếp <tên>, <phẩm> ở <cung>, thỉnh an …"). The higher one does not have to name herself; she uses *bổn cung* (本宫) toward lower ranks and servants. Self-reference changes with the rank of the listener.
2. **Unique titles are public.** Hoàng hậu, Hoàng quý phi (one seat in Qing practice), Thái hậu and Hoàng thượng are known to all by title. Nobody "introduces" them, and a lower one bows on sight.
3. **Heralds.** A eunuch or palace maid announces arrivals (thông truyền: "Hoàng thượng giá lâm", "<phẩm> <tên> đến"). At the morning audience (thỉnh an Hoàng hậu) and at banquets the names are called. So the lower side learns a name without asking.
4. **Proud / cold high ranks** (dramas: Hoa phi in *Hậu cung Chân Hoàn truyện*, Cao Quý phi in *Diên Hi công lược* / *Như Ý truyện*) often answer a lower consort's greeting with a nod or "đứng lên đi" and never give their own name. The lower one learns it from her maid later. Gentle high ranks (Thẩm Mi Trang type, Thuần phi) answer warmly and name themselves.
5. **Equals** exchange names and palaces as sisters ("tỷ tỷ / muội muội"). A cold or proud one keeps aloof.

## What it means for our rule (owner's chosen rule 05:28Z "phẩm + tính cách")
