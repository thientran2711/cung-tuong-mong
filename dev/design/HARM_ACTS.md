## 1. Conventions
- Grades: nhẹ / vừa / nặng (OD 16:40Z). Duration unit: khắc / buổi / tuần (VANPHONG). No % and no numbers here; numbers go to BAL later (ONE CORE).
- Layers (existing order): portrait < face fx (10 pallor/poison, 20 rash, 30 slap) < scar < veil < frame. New fx need a layer type: F = face (cheek box exists), H = hair/head (anchor MISSING), O = outfit (anchor MISSING), W = whole-figure wet/stain overlay (anchor MISSING). KIT REQUEST needed for H, O, W before art.
- "Remove": T = time only, C = change clothes (costs a khắc at home, free), Wa = wash (basin, free), M = ointment to BUY from Thái y (3 tiers, see §4), H = hairdresser / chải lại by a kẻ hầu.
- Who: "Trên→dưới" = same rule as Tát (attacker's rank higher, same place, deny Hoàng thượng/Thái hậu as targets). "Lỡ tay" = anyone (even lower→higher) can do it as a staged accident; chance of being caught; if caught the higher-rank victim punishes. "Lệnh" = ordered through a ma ma / kẻ hầu; the principal bears Đức loss. "Hậu/3 bậc" = Hoàng hậu or three ranks above (same rule as existing Phạt quỳ).
- Confidence of source: C = famous scene in the named drama; T = common trope across palace dramas, no single scene I can cite; R = real Qing/Ming court practice. (Offline research; episode numbers not claimed.)

## 5. Rank rules (one table)
- Open acts (nhẹ, vừa) follow Tát: attacker's rank above target's, same place, target not Hoàng thượng/Thái hậu.
- Heavy acts (nặng): Hoàng hậu or three ranks above (as Phạt quỳ), through a ma ma (Lệnh).
- "Lỡ tay": any rank, the act is staged as an accident. Lower→higher: higher chance of being caught, harsher penalty. This keeps the player able to hurt a higher-rank rival without breaking the rank rule.
- Kẻ hầu as target: only the master's order (Lệnh).
