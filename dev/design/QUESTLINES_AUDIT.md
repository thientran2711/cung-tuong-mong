## 0. Summary (recommendation first)
- **Most of the skeleton is LIVE on main.** NPC roads (OD:L1243/L1252/L1264) are built: every consort, maid, eunuch, court man, house head and grown NPC prince lives a road of his/her own origin, plays it off-screen with his/her own stats, and asks the player for help in exactly ONE chapter per life, rolled per life (`jnroad.js`, `jnroadm.js`, `BAL.nroad`). The Emperor's side story (OD:L1071) is LIVE (`jreign.js`, 6 chapters + faction road), plus 3-step Tâm nguyện lines for the 6 key people. j72 Việc nhờ (108 templates) is LIVE. NPCs plan buổi by buổi (OD:L1601 (1), ruled at L1592) on `review/npc-chain-land` (jagday, landing).
- **What is missing is depth, not structure:**
  1. **G1 — the help hồi is generic** (L644 "real stories", L1601 (2) "cả một cái quá trình"): the request is one of 8 stock cards (nói giúp Hoàng thượng / Thái hậu / Hoàng hậu, đưa thư, dò la, thái y, che chở, bạc); only its header names the NPC's chapter. The text and choices never speak of what that chapter is about.
  2. **G2 — no visible "process"**: the player cannot see the side lines she has joined (whose road, which chapter, what came of her help) — only a news line and one row on the person card.
  3. **G3 — her own road never depends on a helper's nature** (L1601 (5)): 173 «Nhờ …» options in the 9 road files roll on HER stats only; the asked person's traits / tâm cơ / liking play no part; no half-hearted help, refusal, sabotage or telling on her.
  4. **G4 — two-way sabotage is thin** (L1601 (4)): «Đem chuyện mách cho {B}» exists (Đức −3, bond −12, rival +8, her chapter harder) but the betrayed NPC never learns it / strikes back; no reward is tied to how the NPC's road ENDS after her help.
  5. **G5 — NPC grand endings** (L1601 (4), L1613 (5)): designed in GRAND_ENDINGS §2.9, not built (E1 build order step 5). This audit does NOT redesign it; it only names the hooks it needs (§3).
  6. **G6 — stale docs**: ROAD_MAP §0 row "NPC roads … 0 done" (`dev/story/ROAD_MAP.md:20`) and GRAND_ENDINGS §1.5 "NPC roads are not built" are wrong; ROAD_MAP §4.10 (men templates, promised in reports/npc-roads.md:143 for Foreman B's canon check) was never written.
- **Recommended order**: Q0 docs fix (SMALL) → H1 history/drama expert (OD:L1327) → Q1 help-hồi content + Q2 Phụ tuyến ledger + two-way (engine) → Q3 helpers in her road (needs Foreman B, owner pick D-Q1) → Q4 = GE step 5 (NPC parity) reads Q2's hooks.
